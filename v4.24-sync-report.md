# v4.24 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.21 to v4.24, synced 2026-11-08. `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.24.docx` is
Mike's edition of 8 November, read with every tracked change taken as accepted (his instruction of
the same day); its cover still reads v4.23 (below). v4.22 and v4.23 came and went on the shelf
unsynced. Against v4.21 the delta is 1,436 to 2,069 text lines: four new Ancestries with their
Bloodlines (Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Halfling), two new Cultures (Cairnward, Lethra), seven new Skill
Constellations (Crafting, Insight, Medicine, Performance, Society, Survival, Thievery) each with an
Actions table, a Chapter 8 "Actions" reference in three kinds (Maneuvers, Activities, Endeavors),
new Maneuvers (Reload ⓿ to ❻, Recall Knowledge ❶ to ❷, Pass ⓿ once a round, Squeeze ❶), Hustle as
an Activity on Endure, Provision gone from the Endeavors, the Exploration trait, the Calling Roots
opening "You become Trained in this Calling", and Preparing at two actions now in Chapter 2 and the
Prepared trait. Chapter 4 (the Exchange, damage, Vigor, Wounds, Load) is unchanged but for two
typos, so the engine's combat rules did not move. Mike's standing instruction held: plan first
(`lucky-kindling-walrus.md`, approved before the first edit), change after.

Everything downstream moved: `data/*.xlsx` (Mike's restructured workbooks, read as he wrote them;
the five corrections below), `assets/roster.json`, the converter, a new index tool, the builders,
the web app, the Foundry system (0.20.0), its `FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`, `data/README.txt`
and `CLAUDE.md`; `data/SYNC.json` is stamped v4.24.

The numbering continues the 0.19.0 changelog entry's, which ended at 204: this report begins at
205.

## Rulings

205. **Preparing spends two actions now and reserves the rest.** `SW.PREPARE_SPEND` (2) in
     `module/config/actions.mjs`; `prepare()` spends it and reserves the cost less it (a Committed
     Strike ❸: two spent, one reserved; a ❹ Cast: two and two), the card and the sheet strings say
     two, Abandon returns the reserve and names what was lost. Chapter 4's procedure and the
     Preparing condition are brought to two by the redline
     `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.24_preparing_proposal.docx`, which also brings the cover to
     v4.24. Through 0.19.0 the system spent one (PHB v4.10 to v4.21).
206. **The row's Type types an action; the `_Tree Index` is read when present and never required;
     `assets/index_sheets.py` keeps every index current.** An actions workbook is an index with a
     Name column and no Tree column; its Type column is any header containing "type" (`Type`,
     `Action Type`; `Category/Type` is the Category). A row's Type cell, else the index row, else
     the sheet name; the "not in _Tree Index" warning fires only for a row with no Type cell. A
     row's book group is its own Category cell else its sheet, never the index's Category column
     (Mike's hand-kept "Athletics Action" there would have split the Athletics sheet in two on the
     Actions tab, as the first live drive showed). **An index name with no row on any sheet retires
     a roster row of the name** exactly as a blank sheet row did through 0.19.0 (ruling 61): Mike
     deleted the blank Parry, Void, Counter, Intercept and Avert Gaze rows and kept the names, and
     without this the five roster rows shipped again (the first drive showed Parry and Void on
     Tess's Actions tab); `actions.json` carries them as `retired`. The tool adds a row for every
     unindexed sheet or action with its Category, leaves hand-kept cells blank and named, reports
     a row whose sheet or row is gone with the nearest name, never drops or overwrites, backs up to
     `backup/index-<date>/`, and saves only on a change. This run: 8 rows into `activities.xlsx`,
     2 into `endeavors.xlsx`, 35 into `maneuvers.xlsx`, 7 into `skills.xlsx`; the five names above
     reported; Murovian reported in `cultures.xlsx`.
207. **Every ungranted Maneuver is basic.** A Maneuver, Activity or Endeavor is on every
     character's Actions tab unless its Type is `Talent Maneuver` or a Talent's `[MANEUVER X]` rule
     or `@Maneuver[X]` marker names it (every tree, enabled or not, and the Backgrounds). Mike's
     sheets type seventeen granted rows as plain "Maneuver"; the build reads them as granted and
     names them each run (Steady the Line, Quench the Ember, Glimmer, Quick Tongue, Oathbound,
     Tumbling Retreat, Kip Up, Sure Seat, Bull Rush, Reliable Anchor, Rally, Talk Them Down, Command
     the Field, Feinting Step, Battle Cry, No Quarter, Lost in the Dark). Climb, Hide, Grapple,
     Feint and the other Skill actions stay on every sheet as the v4.21 Encounter rows did.
208. **The Lore template is skills.xlsx's `Lore (LORE_NAME)` sheet.** The converter reads the
     index's `Lore` row from a sheet named `Lore (…)` and strips the `(LORE_NAME)` placeholder from
     the sheet's Talent names, Effects, Automation and requirements and from an action's name and
     Constellation cell (Well-Read on the Lore Actions sheet). The template is "Lore" with Deep
     Knowledge, Practical Application and Well-Read, the book's three, under the ids they had.
     `data/lore.xlsx` is deleted (Mike).
209. **Activities and Endeavors are rows of their workbooks, and any row typed Activity or Endeavor
     is a Mode Item wherever it sits.** `data/activities.xlsx` (the eight) and `data/endeavors.xlsx`
     (Retrain, Train) replace the roster's hand-kept `explorationActions` and `downtimeActions`
     blocks, which are gone. An Activity is an Exploration Mode Item (category "Exploration Mode",
     key `exploration:<slug>`, so a character's stored Activity id still resolves), an Endeavor a
     Downtime Mode one (`downtime:<slug>`), on the Skill sheets too (Treat Wounds and Survey
     Workmanship are Activities, Train Animal and Convalescent Care Endeavors), neither spending
     actions (`passive`). An Activity's road data comes from its Movement Speed, Check and
     Initiative columns (the last two added this release and filled from the book's Effects: Hustle
     Endure / Endure, Search Awareness / Awareness, Avoid Notice Stealth / Stealth, Look Harmless
     Guile / Guile, Investigate choice / choice, the others blank), Scout's and Defend's effects by
     name; an Endeavor's Duration is `system.duration`, the Downtime panel's clock. Hustle rolls
     Endure (Athletics for Initiative through v4.21).
210. **The Constellation column is the check a row rolls, and the Defense is read from the
     Effect.** When the cell names exactly one Constellation or Defense and the Effect attempts or
     rolls a check, `system.check` is enabled on it (Climb Athletics, Hide Stealth); the Defense
     when the Effect names exactly one after "vs." or "against" (Demoralize's Guard, Talk Them
     Down's Guard), so Iron Etiquette's Intimidation line has a shipped Maneuver to fire on. "N/A",
     a list ("Melee or Ranged"), a blank, or an Effect that rolls nothing (Steady the Line's Human)
     leaves the check off; "Relevant Check" opens the picker (Aid, Recall Knowledge). A GM may
     edit the field on a world Item as before.
211. **Provision is gone.** The book's Endeavors are Retrain and Train; the Downtime panel's
     Provision hand line, `PROVISION` and `Travel.provisionByHand` go with the row. Chapter 4's
     Downtime Mode paragraph still names Provisioning; the consistency proposal strikes it.
212. **The Necromancer's Opening Vigor is 8.** Mike's Root cell says `[VIGOR opening 8]` and the
     Chapter 3 table says 8; the root text's "you also gain 6 Vigor" is the defect (the consistency
     proposal brings it to 8). Ruling 152's 6 is superseded; the roster row and the smoke test read
     8.
213. **Supine is not implemented.** The Conditions table adds it with an empty Effects cell, and
     Drop Prone and Stand name it; the system has Prone alone until the book says what Supine does.
214. **Look Harmless's Reach limit is 1.** The warning on the road reads a held weapon's Reach
     above 1 (the book's "greater than 1"; "greater than Adjacent", Reach above 0, through v4.21).
