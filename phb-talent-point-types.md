# Types of Talent Points

Two versions below. The **drop-in** is just the two missing bullets, if that is all you want today.
The **rewrite** fixes a structural problem in the section and documents three point types the book
uses but never defines.

---

# 1. Drop-in: the two missing bullets

> - **Comets** are Talent Points with no strings attached. A Comet may be spent on any Constellation
>   you qualify for, open or not, Flared or not. You gain 3 at character creation and 1 at every
>   level-up.
> - **Milestone Talent Points** may only be spent in a **Flared** Constellation. You gain 1 at each
>   of the first three Milestones of every level, and each must be spent the moment you receive it.

---

# 2. The rewrite, and why

## The problem with the current list

The list mixes two different questions and presents them as one kind of thing.

**Lore, Skill and Opening answer "where may this go?"** They are restrictions on the target.

**Comets and Milestone points answer something else entirely: "does the Constellation have to be
Flared?"** Neither one restricts where the point goes. A Milestone point can be spent anywhere at
all, as long as that Constellation is lit. A Comet can be spent anywhere, full stop.

So a reader who has just learned that Lore points go on Lore Constellations will reasonably expect
Comet points to go on Comet Constellations, and there is no such thing. Sorting the list on the
right axis fixes that in one table.

## Three types the book uses and never defines

The character creation table hands out **1 Origin**, **1 Calling**, and **1 Defense** point, and
talents elsewhere grant more (*Driven* gives a Calling Talent Point, *Quick Study* gives a Skill
Talent Point). None of those three appear in *Types of Talent Points*. A player following the
chargen table will meet a term the rules section never introduced.

## Replacement text

> ## Types of Talent Points
>
> Every Talent costs exactly 1 Talent Point, but not every Talent Point is free to go anywhere.
> Specific nomenclature is used to explain the guard rails concisely. A Talent Point answers two
> questions: **where it may be spent**, and **whether the Constellation must be Flared.**
>
> ### Where it may be spent
>
> | Type | May be spent on |
> |---|---|
> | **Origin Talent Point** | Your Origin Constellation only |
> | **Calling Talent Point** | Calling Constellations only |
> | **Defense Talent Point** | The four Defense Constellations only: Awareness, Evade, Guard, Endure |
> | **Skill Talent Point** | Skill Constellations only |
> | **Lore Talent Point** | Lore Constellations only |
> | **Opening Talent Point** | Opening a new Constellation only, whatever kind. It buys a Root Talent and nothing else |
> | **Comet** | Anything you qualify for |
>
> ### Whether the Constellation must be Flared
>
> | Type | Flare required? | When you get it |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Milestone Talent Point** | **Yes.** It must go into a Flared Constellation | 1 at each of the first three Milestones of every level |
> | **Comet** | **No.** Open or not, Flared or not | 3 at character creation, and 1 at every level-up |
> | Every point granted at character creation, or by a Talent | No | As stated where it is granted |
>
> ### Three rules that apply to all of them
>
> **They all count toward your Proficiency Rank.** A point is a point once it is spent. A Lore point
> that bought a Lore root counts toward Expert in that Lore exactly as a Comet would.
>
> **They all feed an Attribute.** Every Talent adds 1 Attribute Point to its Attribute no matter
> which kind of Talent Point paid for it. There is no such thing as a free Talent that does not
> shape who you are.
>
> **None of them may be banked.** You spend a Talent Point the moment you receive it. If a
> restricted point has no legal target at all, and only then, it becomes a Comet.

# 3. Notes on the choices above

**"If a restricted point has no legal target, it becomes a Comet."** This rule does not currently
exist and you will need it at the table. An **Opening Talent Point** is the case that will actually
come up: it may only open a new Constellation, and a character can reach a point where every
Constellation they qualify for is already open. The rule says points are never banked, so without
this clause the point simply evaporates and a player loses something they earned. Converting to a
Comet is the generous reading and it costs nothing, because the situation is rare by construction.

**Comets are listed on both axes**, deliberately. A Comet is defined by having no restriction of
either kind, so it belongs in both tables as the "none of the above" row. That repetition is a
feature: it is the answer to both questions a player is asking.

**Milestone points are the only entry on the Flare axis that says yes**, which makes the design
visible in one glance: the Flare requirement is not a general property of Talent Points, it is the
specific price of the three points your play earns you between levels. That is your third axiom
made into a table.

**I left "Comet" as a bare noun** rather than renaming it "Comet Talent Point" for consistency with
the others. It is already a Key Term, it is the one evocative word in the set, and "spend a Comet"
reads better than "spend a Comet Talent Point." Worth a deliberate decision rather than drift.

# 4. Three small things I noticed while checking this

**The chargen table says "3 Free" in the Talent Points column** where every other row names its
type. It should say **3 Comets**, so that the word a player looks up is the word they were given.

**Hero Points are now consistent, and the Trained restriction is gone from both places.** Ch. 2 and
Key Terms both read "reroll a check." That resolves SW-011. Worth knowing what it cost: the
restriction was the one mechanical difference between Untrained and Trained other than the +4, so
raw talent can now be rescued by fate exactly as well as training can. If that was deliberate,
nothing to do.

**Retraining is still a month in one place and a month in the other**, which is good, but the
Level-Up Checklist says "spend your Comet" while the Comets section under character creation is
empty (p774 is a heading with no body). Whatever you were going to put there is still missing.
