# v4.10 sync report

Player's Handbook v3.4 to v4.10, synced 2026-09-28. This is not a point release of the rules: the
combat engine is rewritten (six actions a round spent across Opportunities, the Exchange, the Bind,
Vigor and Wounds), the check formula drops level, the rank ladder and the Attribute divisor change,
Weapons splits into the Melee and Ranged parent Constellations, and Actions become Maneuvers with
new glyphs. Everything downstream moved: `data/*.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`, the web app, the
Foundry system (0.4.0), its `FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook is ambiguous, contradicts itself, or leaves
something to the implementation. Each one is a place the handbook could be tightened.

## Rulings

1. **Attribute Bonus divisor: 4.** Character Mechanics (the formula, the table 0-3/4-7/8-11) and
   Finishing Up both say points ÷ 4. Key Terms still says "divided by 3, rounded down, to a maximum
   of +5", which is the v3.4 sentence. Implemented ÷ 4. The +5 maximum is kept, because nothing in
   v4.10 repeals it and the Key Terms sentence is the only place a cap has ever been stated; the new
   table's "every 4 more, +1 more" reads as open-ended, so this is the ruling to confirm.
2. **The check formula has no level term.** "Level opens the door to higher ranks and pays out
   Talent Points; it never touches the die." Thresholds are 10 + Attribute + Proficiency. Every
   check, Attack Threshold and Defense Threshold in the system drops its level term. Adversary
   Thresholds typed on a sheet are unchanged, since they were always the GM's number.
3. **Ranks are +3 / +6 / +9 / +12, gated at levels 1 / 5 / 10 / 15**, at 1 / 4 / 9 / 16 points.
   Untrained is +0. The Defenses section adds "every Threshold that depends on it is 3 lower" for an
   Untrained Defense, which is the same arithmetic stated from the other side.
4. **Two Defenses at creation, not four.** Chapter 3 grants 2 Defense Talent Points. Key Terms still
   defines Defense as "one of four constellations every character begins play Trained in"; that is
   v3.4 text. Implemented: two, the player's choice, in the wizard.
5. **Melee Training or Ranged Training is granted at creation** ("1 is automatically granted:
   Melee Training or Ranged Training, your choice"). The wizard asks which.
6. **Parent Constellations inherit rank only.** Every Talent bought in a Combat Style counts toward
   its parent's points (Melee: Two-Weapon, Shield, Brawling, Dueling, Great Weapon, Spear & Polearm,
   Armored; Ranged: Archery, Crossbow, Missile Skirmishing). The parent's own Talents must still be
   bought. The parent's Attribute Points do NOT inherit: a Talent feeds the Attribute it names, once.
   Data: a `Parent` column in the `_Tree Index` of `combat_styles.xlsx`.
7. **Speed is in feet per Move, and a Human's is 6.** Step is half Speed (3 ft for a Human),
   rounded down. Rush is five times Speed in a straight line, less Load Strain in feet. Speed 25 in
   the data was the v3.4 number; every chassis speed is re-read from the book.
8. **Weapon dice by level: 2 at 4th, 3 at 8th, 4 at 12th, 5 at 16th** (Equipment and the Level-Up
   Checklist agree; v3.4 had 4/12/19).
9. **The Multiple Attack Penalty is gone.** No sentence in v4.10 imposes one; tempo is paid in
   actions instead. The engine's MAP ladder and its per-turn attack counter are removed.
10. **Aid in `data/actions.xlsx` differs from the handbook**, and the sheet wins by the standing rule
    (2026-09-26): the sheet's Aid is a single action with a Relevant Check; the book's is Prepare ❶
    plus a ⓿↺ Reaction against Threshold 15. Flagged for the author, not changed here.
11. **"Varisians" appears in the Cultures introduction** ("an orc raised among Varisians"). That is
    a Golarion proper noun; the shipping data says Serrovane and Kestrel Reach. Not carried across.
12. **Sneak's Trait line reads "Move"**, which is the retired name of the Motion trait. Carried
    across as Motion.
13. **A parent's rank counts its children's points only once the parent's Root is owned.** "Every
    Talent you buy in a child counts toward your Proficiency Rank in its parent" read literally
    would make a character Trained in Ranged from Archery alone, which contradicts "buying the Root
    is what makes you Trained." The Root-first rule governs: without Melee Training the Melee rank
    is Untrained whatever the Combat Styles hold; with it, every child point counts.
14. **Specialization follows the weapon's parent**: Melee rank for a weapon in hand, Ranged for
    one that leaves it. Tier gates in a parent are met by the pooled rank (rank is rank).
15. **Any Hit on a Spent creature Wounds it, a Quick Strike's included.** Chapter 2 excludes the
    Quick Strike only from the Exposed-Zone critical trigger; "any Hit lands while you are Spent" is
    the second trigger and has no exception.
16. **Half of an odd Speed rounds down** (Math Conventions): a Speed 5 creature Steps 2 feet.
17. **Rush ❸ is Prepared by the Traits table** ("A Maneuver of three or more actions"), though its
    own entry is silent. The system charges a Rush-length drag three actions at once and leaves
    the telegraphing to the table, because a drag is a move already made; the report notes it.
18. **A full circuit of Passes ends the round in the system**, as Encounter Mode says; the GM's
    own Next Round control still works. Pass is a tracker and sheet control first; it is also
    printed in the Maneuvers compendium because Chapter 7 lists "Pass ⓿" in its table, even
    though Chapter 2 says "Pass is not a Maneuver at all". Using the compendium row posts the
    card and nothing else; the control is what counts the Pass.
19. **"Expose a plausible Zone" is a picker the attacker may use**, never a rule the engine
    resolves: GM judgement inside a formula cannot be automated (the working agreement's standing
    risk). Chapter 7 says "may"; Chapter 2's table reads as mandatory; the picker honours the
    weaker reading and the GM can veto on the card.
20. **A Bind ends when the Controlling actor is attacked by a third party at the moment the
    attack is declared** (Chapter 2: "when the Controlling actor is attacked"), not only if it
    lands.
21. **Gain Control's Miss hands over Control and nothing more**: its own entry governs (Chapter 2,
    Other Attacks), and Control already yields an Exposed Zone.
22. **Wounded [Zone] is four counters on the actor and one token status**, not four statuses:
    the sheet shows each Zone's Wounds against its capacity; the token shows that a Wound exists.
23. **The roster's Postures block is the Reaction table now.** The handbook removed the Postures
    table in favour of Talent-granted Reactions ⓿↺; Give Ground and Set Your Feet survive as the
    Postures Evade Training and Guard Training grant, so nothing authored was deleted. Parry, Void,
    Counter and Intercept are printed by the book both as Reactions and as Defense & Recovery
    Maneuvers; the compendium ships each once, as a Maneuver, under its old document id.
23a. **The level-scaled "Task level" Threshold table is emptied.** It was in neither v3.4 nor v4.10
    (it dated from the v1.1 baseline, when a check carried a level term), and with level off the
    die a Threshold of 40 at level 20 is unreachable. The roster key stays, as an empty list, so
    its readers survive; restoring it is the author's call.
23b. **Loose and Move moved from Archery to Ranged.** Its compendium id changes with its
    Constellation (`talent:archery:loose-and-move` to `talent:ranged:loose-and-move`); a character
    holding the Archery copy keeps a working Item, and the system files it under Ranged when it
    prepares the Talent (`MOVED_TALENTS` in `config.mjs`), so its point counts where the book now
    says.
24. **Parry, Void, Counter and Intercept appear on every character's Maneuvers tab** as the
    book's Defense & Recovery Maneuvers do; using one without its Training Talent is announced,
    not prevented, like everything else.
25. **Reload [N] has no definition in v4.10** (the trait row is empty). The roster prints "Reloading
    takes N actions", which is what every Reload talent assumes.
26. **The Step a Talent grants inside another Maneuver or Reaction is ⓿** unless the Talent prices
    it; a priced Step at the same Opportunity would be a second Maneuver, which the rules forbid.

27. **The creation step table still prints "1 Defense"** (table 39, row 6) while the Defenses
    section and Finishing Up both say 2 Defense Talent Points. Implemented 2.
28. **The Level-Up Checklist still gates Master at 13 and Legendary at 19**; the Chapter 2 rank
    table says 10 and 15 and is the rule the ranks are defined by. Implemented 10 and 15.
29. **Parry (weapon trait) is a Gear bonus in v4.10**, overriding the 2026-09-26 ruling that weapon
    traits are Situation. The book wins. It shares the Gear type with a Raised shield's bonus, so
    the two do not stack: a shield fighter with a Parry blade gets the higher of the two.

30. **A Zone already at its Wound capacity that takes another Wound while the creature is not
    Dying begins Dying again** (1, or 2 from a Critical Hit); while Dying, damage raises the Dying
    value instead. The book's final-Wound effect is "You are Dying", and Recovery, Healing and
    Refusing Death all end Dying while "your Wounds remain", so the final Wound must be able to
    put you down twice.
31. **Intercept is announced when the entering token lands**, not before the movement. Table 8
    says a Move-triggered Reaction happens before the movement; Intercept and Measure say "at the
    moment they enter". A drag is a move already made, so the card offers the Intercept the
    instant the move commits, and the table decides whether it lands before the last step.
32. **The Example of Play does its sums at Trained +4** ("2 + 4, so d20+6") against the book's
    +3 ladder, and has Recenter clear a Zone Exposed by a Posture, which the rules forbid four
    times over. The rules text governs; the example is flagged for the author.

33. **Two Defense Talent Points open two different Defenses.** "You begin play Trained in two of
    them, your choice"; both the wizard and the app refuse the second point on the same Defense.
34. **Endure's Load Strain relief is Expert 1, Master 2, Legendary 3**, as the Endure Training
    Talent has read since v3.2. The web app's table had Trained 1 / Expert 2 / Master 3 /
    Legendary 4, a bug since v3.2 that this sync corrected.
35. **Tracked Wounds apply their effects to derived numbers** in both the app and the Foundry
    system: a Torso Wound is Off-Guard, an Arms Wound is −2 Situation to Guard and to attacks, a
    Legs Wound halves Speed (each Wound short of the final halves what is left, per "halves what
    Speed it had left"), and a Head Wound blocks Reactions. The first Wound's effect stays in force
    when a later one lands.
36. **Fatigued is a Condition penalty** ("something on you"), so it does not stack with Frightened.
37. **A held shield satisfies Parry's rigid-implement requirement** whether or not it is Raised;
    a Natural Weapons weapon is a bare hand and is not rigid.
38. **Only an explicit Pass counts toward the circuit** in the system; a GM clicking Next Turn
    without Passing breaks the streak, because a round ended early cannot be undone. A combatant
    that cannot act (Defeated, Unconscious, Dying) Passes by necessity when its Opportunity is
    skipped.
39. **A straight line, for Rush, is a path whose grid cost equals its direct edge measure**, so
    exact diagonals are straight; a straight drag longer than 3 × Speed and within Rush is charged
    as a Rush ❸, and one longer than Rush falls back to Moves.
40. **An adversary's Intercept is a Defense roll by the mover** against the attack's Threshold (the
    players roll everything), and the adversary pays ❶.
41. **A stored Speed of 15 or more is a v3.4 value** and migrates as Speed ÷ 4 (25 becomes 6).
    The conversion runs once, as a world migration the first GM to open the world performs, and is
    recorded in the `systemVersion` world setting; it is not a rule in `migrateData`, which Foundry
    runs on every load and every update and which would have quartered any v4.10 Speed of 15 or
    more for ever (review finding, 2026-10-01).

The rulings below were found in the code's own comments during the documentation pass and are
numbered on from the implementation agents' list.

42. **A Torso Wound's 1d4 bleed runs at the end of every round while the Wound stands**, and the
    ordinary Endure check that ends Persistent Damage does not close it; treating the Wound does.
    The Wounds table makes the first Torso Wound "Off-Guard, and 1d4 persistent bleed" and says a
    Wound's effect "does not clear when you Recenter, only when the Wound is treated". The
    end-of-round card for it therefore carries no Endure button (`combat.mjs`,
    `persistentDamageOf`).
43. **Counter stands on the better of the two basic Defenses that are usable right now.** The book
    gives Counter no Defense of its own ("no bonus to your Defense"), so the roll reads whichever
    of Evade and Guard has the higher Threshold and is not unavailable (`helpers/stance.mjs`,
    `documents/actor.mjs` `answeringDefense`). The data model's chip marker still points at Guard
    for a Counter stance; the roll is what governs.
44. **A treated Wound comes off the Zone.** The book binds it (its effect ends, it still counts
    for a week); the system decrements the Zone's count on a successful Treat and says on the card
    that the effect has ended. A bound-but-counted Wound would need a second per-Zone field, and
    the sync brief asked for the simpler reading (`documents/actor.mjs`, `treatWound`).
45. **A Talent filed under the retired Weapons slug refiles under Melee** on first load, because a
    weapon in hand is where the Weapons Constellation's rolls went; a character whose training was
    really Ranged moves the Talent by hand (`data/item.mjs`, `SwTalentData.migrateData`). A
    Constellation Item still carrying the Weapons slug on a character is ignored when the skies
    are drawn, so no empty Weapons sky appears beside Melee (`data/actor.mjs`,
    `#prepareConstellations`).
46. **A Reaction is granted by owning its Training Root, not by an inherited rank.** Ten points of
    Dueling make you Trained in Melee once Melee Training is bought, but Counter and Intercept
    arrive with the Root itself, since only rank is inherited and the parent's Talents must be
    bought for their effects (`data/actor.mjs`, `#prepareReactions`).
47. **A Prepared card is public.** "Preparation is telegraphed by design", so the Finish / Abandon
    card at the owner's next Opportunity goes to the whole table; the buttons still check
    ownership (`combat.mjs`, `postPreparedCard`).
48. **A lone combatant's Pass is a whole circuit and ends the round**, and any other Next Turn or
    End Opportunity for it (after a Strike, say) is simply its next Opportunity in the same round,
    because core's turn order would otherwise hand out six fresh actions and expire its Prepared
    Maneuver every click (`combat.mjs`, `SwCombat#nextTurn`, `registerPass`).
49. **Damage that is not a Strike ignores Exposure.** A Blast or a fall (`strike: null` in the
    damage pipeline) meets a Zone's full Protection and never Wounds through an Exposed Zone; only
    a Deliberate or Committed Strike does (`data/actor.mjs`, `zoneProtection`).
50. **A Committed Strike outside an encounter rolls at once.** Prepared means something only where
    Opportunities exist, so with no combat running the three actions are noted and the die is
    thrown (`documents/actor.mjs`, `rollAttack`).

The rulings below come from the adversarial review of the sync (2026-10-01, 44 findings, 40
confirmed), where a finding turned on a reading of the book rather than a plain defect.

51. **A Quick Strike that Misses Exposes the attacker.** Table 9's Quick row says "Nothing. You
    recover your line at once." and Key Terms says "a Quick Strike never", but the sentence after
    Table 9 ("A Miss Exposes you regardless of commitment"), the Result table, Reading the Result
    ("whatever the Strike was") and the Exposed section ("when any Strike of yours Misses") all say
    otherwise, and the sync brief specified Miss always. Implemented the Result's reading; the
    card's Expose button is an offer the defender may decline, and the kinds table carries the
    flag (`STRIKE_KINDS.exposeOnMiss` in `config.mjs`, read by `dice/check.mjs`) so one cell flips
    it if the author rules the other way.
52. **A Strike made as a Reaction meets the basic Defense only.** "A Reaction never triggers
    another Reaction: you cannot Counter a Counter or an Intercept, and a fighter who is Countered
    rolls only their basic Defense against it." The book states it for Counter and Intercept; the
    riposte is a Reaction's Quick Strike too and is treated the same. The target's stance is not
    read, nothing is charged to them, and no Counter is offered back (`documents/actor.mjs`,
    `rollAttack`). The mirror case holds for the players' side: a mover answering an adversary's
    Intercept rolls the basic Defense alone, with no Parry, Void or Counter offered in the dialog
    (`documents/actions.mjs`, `onInterceptDefense`; `basicOnly` in `dice/check.mjs`).
53. **Counter answers only a melee Blow from a foe within your Reach.** An arrow, or a spear from
    beyond your Reach, at a Counter stance meets the better basic Defense, charges nothing, and
    leaves the stance standing; the card says so. Parry against a ranged Blow is left to the table
    as before ("Guard answers a ranged Blow only with a shield Raised" is a judgement about what
    was raised), so only Counter is gated (`documents/actor.mjs`, `answeringDefense`).
54. **Only a bindable attack is offered a Bind.** A Bind is "when a rigid weapon or shield stops a
    melee weapon or natural attack"; Flexible weapons, Unparryable attacks and anything that left
    the hand cannot be Bound. The card offers Form Bind / Take Control only for such an attack, and
    records both weapons (the defender's implement and the weapon that was Stopped) so the
    Controlled −2 and the "attacking with another weapon ends the Bind" rule follow the right
    weapon (`dice/check.mjs`, `#position`).
55. **The web app gates parent rank on the Root too** (ruling 13 upheld on both siblings): Dueling
    Training alone no longer makes a character Trained in Melee in the app, and the Reactions
    checklist ticks Counter and Intercept from Melee Training, not from a derived rank (ruling 46).
    The app's migration also files a v3.4 Archery "Loose and Move" under Ranged (the talent moves;
    the point is not freed) and logs every talent or constellation it drops.
56. **An adversary's profile lists only its own Reaction abilities.** Parry, Void, Counter and
    Intercept are the GM's to declare for any adversary, so they stay live on the stance chips and
    the Intercept card, but the "Reactions it has" panel no longer claims all four for every
    creature (`apps/npc-sheet.mjs`).
57. **A bow in hand is a rigid implement.** The brief's definition ("a weapon without the Flexible
    trait, or a shield") is the one the data model and the i18n text carry, and the Actor
    document's getter now agrees instead of excluding ranged weapons on its own
    (`documents/actor.mjs`, `rigidImplement`). If bows should not Parry, the single place to say so
    is `SwWeaponData.prepareDerivedData`.
58. **Exploration and Downtime Activities are passive, not free Maneuvers.** They were built with
    the v3 cost key "0", which v4.10 redefined as ⓿; they take the same cost path as every other
    glyph-less name (`build_foundry.mjs`), and a 0.3.7 world copy of one is migrated to passive by
    its category (`data/item.mjs`, `migrateCost`).
59. **A character at Speed 0 Crawls.** The final Legs Wound sets Speed 0 ("You fall Prone and
    cannot Stand"); a drag is then charged as Crawl ❶ per 3 feet, never provokes an Intercept, and
    the ruler says Crawl. An adversary's Legs Wounds are read from its derived `moveSpeed`, since
    its stored Speed stays the creature's own for the sheet (`documents/actions.mjs`,
    `movementNumbers`, `classifyMove`).

## Questions for the author

The cataloguers' full list of ambiguities (over a hundred, most of them talent-level) is kept in
the session's catalogue files and summarised here by theme. Each is a place a sentence in the
handbook would settle it.

- **Two "Prepared" verbs.** Aid says "Prepare ❶" for a one-action Maneuver; Prepared is also the
  trait of three-action Maneuvers. The system does not set Preparing for Aid.
- **Indivisible versus abandonable.** Chapter 7 says a two-action Maneuver interrupted is lost
  entirely; Chapter 2 lets a Prepared Maneuver be abandoned with the reserve returned. Which
  governs a ❷ Maneuver interrupted by a Reaction?
- **Quick Strikes and unreachable critical clauses.** Several ❶ Talents print a Critical Success
  rider a Quick Strike with a non-Agile weapon can never reach (Brutish Shove, Sleek Reposition,
  Shoulder Check). Either they inherit the ❶ to ❸ range or the rider is decorative.
- **"Drops a creature"** has no meaning in the Vigor and Wounds vocabulary (Spent, unconscious, or
  Dying?).
- **Hafted, Flourish, Spear & Polearm weapon** are used as traits or categories but defined
  nowhere.
- **Armored Fighting's Key Attribute** is still Presence in the heading and Might in the table.
- **Dueling Parry versus Parry**: both +2 Situation; only Control and the riposte distinguish them.
- **Heedless** has no duration.
- **Reactive Strike's Intercept extension** and Punishing Blow imply a disruption baseline Intercept
  does not state.
- **Key Terms still says ÷ 3, four Trained Defenses, and "Weighted" only for Committed Strikes**;
  the Cultures paragraph names Varisians.
- **Table 9's Quick row ("Nothing. You recover your line at once.") and Key Terms ("a Quick Strike
  never") contradict the sentence after Table 9, the Result table and the Exposed section**, which
  all Expose the attacker on any Miss. Ruling 51 implements the majority reading; one cell either
  way.
- **The Wounds table says "You cannot use Reactions" for a Head Wound and the Stopped rules say a
  Reaction never triggers a Reaction**, but neither says what a riposte (a Reaction's Quick Strike)
  meets. Ruling 52 reads it as the basic Defense, like Counter and Intercept.

## What moved, by artifact

### Data (`data/*.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`, `assets/sheet_spec.json`)

- `data/weapons.xlsx`: the Weapons sheet is gone. Two sheets replace it, **Melee** (Weapon •
  Might: Melee Training, Weapon Familiarity, Quick Draw, Turn the Weapon, Sure Grip, Read the
  Steel ❶, Reactive Strike, Feeling, Winding, Master Cut) and **Ranged** (Weapon • Agility: Ranged
  Training, Aim ❶, Loose and Move, Steady Release). Loose and Move moved here from Archery.
- `data/combat_styles.xlsx`: a `Parent` column in `_Tree Index`, seven Styles under Melee and three
  under Ranged; 61 talent rows became 60 with Loose and Move gone; 25 glyph renames and 48 effects
  rewritten to the v4.10 text.
- The other workbooks: 8 rows added, none deleted, 33 glyph renames, 69 effects rewritten. The
  ancestries index header is Vigor; the Human's Speed is 6 (feet per Move).
- `assets/roster.json`: Maneuvers regrouped as the book groups them (Motion; Attack; Defense &
  Recovery; Watching, Deceiving & Helping; Handling Things; Special) with v4.10 glyphs and text:
  Move, Rush, Gain Control, Close, Parry, Void, Counter, Intercept and Pass added; Stride, Delay and
  Ready removed. `postures` is the Reaction table (Parry, Void, Counter, Intercept, Posture, Give
  Ground, Set Your Feet; the second column reads "Granting Talent • Defense +N"). Conditions carry
  the v4.10 list (Spent, Bound, Controlled, Controlling, Preparing, Wounded [Zone]). The trait
  tables carry Motion, Weighted, Prepared, Flexible, Massive and Unparryable, Parry as +1 Gear, the
  Unwieldy Intercept clause and Reload as "takes N actions". Flail, Whip and Chain Flail are
  Flexible. Handling reads "Melee or Ranged". `damageOrder` says Vigor; `protectionSteps` step 1
  carries the Deliberate-or-Committed rule; `recovery` and `detection` are the v4.10 text;
  `chargenSteps` says 2 Defense and carries the granted Training row; the `defenses` block names
  each Defense's threats; the ancestries block carries `vigor` (and `hp` for one release);
  `thresholdTable` is an empty list. Pass ⓿ also ships as a Defense & Recovery row, so ruling 18's
  "not a compendium document" describes the tracker control rather than the pack.
- `assets/sheet_spec.json`: the printed sheet's labels and formulas moved to v4.10 (Vigor and
  Spent, Wounds per Zone with both effects, six actions and Reserved, Speed / Step / Rush / Leap,
  Melee and Ranged, Strikes, Reactions, Bind).

### Converter and build (`assets/*.py`, `assets/*.mjs`, `assets/*.js`)

- `xlsx_to_trees.py`: validates `Parent` (must name a tree in the book, only one level of
  inheritance, never itself) and writes `parent` into `trees.json`; accepts Vigor or HP as the
  ancestry header and writes both keys into the roster block; parses ⓿ ❶ to ❻, ↺ and a bracketed
  Reaction cost such as "(⓿↺)" alongside the v3 ◆ and ◇; strips every glyph and the trailing cost
  joiner for Requires matching and slugs; writes `reaction` and `reactionCost` into `actions.json`.
- `build_foundry.mjs`: carries `parent` onto the Constellation Item (as `parentSlug`) and the
  content index; writes the Reactions folder from `postures`, skipping the four that are also
  Maneuvers; keeps a sheet action's document id when it takes over a roster row; reads Vigor from
  either key; the Maneuvers pack is labelled "Maneuvers & Activities". Output: 31 Constellations,
  177 Talents, 13 chassis, 29 weapons, 20 armor pieces, 3 shields, 53 Maneuvers and Activities, 20
  reference pages, 5 macros.
- `render_constellations.py`: the ring labels say Expert L5, Master L10, Legendary L15; a glyph the
  plate font lacks (⓿) is swapped for its nearest kin.
- `build_phb.js`: a "Rank Math, Attributes and Checks" section (ranks, parents, ÷ 4, the check
  formula without level), a Reactions table from `postures`, a "Melee, Ranged and the Combat
  Styles" section, a "Vigor, Wounds and Dying" appendix, an introduction to the Maneuvers appendix,
  Shields and Weapons paragraphs, parents in the Combat Style headers, glyph runs in Segoe UI
  Symbol, and the Task-level table printed only when the roster has rows (it has none).
- `sheet_gen.py`: rebuilt for v4.10 (see PDFs below). `smoke_test.js`: new steps for the wizard's
  pools, the Melee and Ranged parents, the Maneuvers wiki rename, and a sweep for v3 vocabulary.
- `data/README.txt`: the `Parent` column, the Vigor header, Speed in feet per Move, the v4.10
  glyphs and their v3 fallbacks.
