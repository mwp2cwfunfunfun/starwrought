# v4.12 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.11 to v4.12, synced 2026-10-02, the same day as v4.11. A point release of the
rules. Mike accepted the wind-exemption proposal made with `assets/phb_propose.py` into his working
copy (`Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.11_wind_proposal.docx`), made edits of his own, and saved it
with no tracked changes left; the numbered edition `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.12.docx` was
written from that file with `assets/phb_edit.py`, correcting one clause that had been saved the
wrong way round (ruling 74). The book moved in three places: the Wind bullet of Load and Load
Strain, the Fatigued row of the Conditions table, and Rage's aftermath. Mike also enabled Endure
Training in `data/defenses.xlsx`, so the Endure Constellation ships to Foundry with its Root alone.
Everything downstream moved: the Berserker sheet of `data/callings.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`,
`assets/sheet_spec.json`, the web app, the Foundry system (0.6.1), its `FEATURES.md` and
`CHANGELOG.md`, both READMEs and `CLAUDE.md`. No world migration: nothing stored changes shape.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook leaves something to the implementation, and
the questions for Mike. The numbering continues the v4.11 report's, which ended at 73.

## What the book now says

Quoted exactly from the v4.12 edition, because every ruling below interprets one of these sentences.

- Chapter 5, Load and Load Strain, the Wind bullet: "Wind. If your Endure Threshold is less than 10
  + your Load Strain, then at the end of the third round of an encounter and every round after, you
  must roll Endure against 10 + Load Strain. On a failure your Fatigued rises by 1." The bullet no
  longer defines Fatigued (that is the Conditions table's job) and has lost its two flavour
  sentences. The v4.11 "Load Strain 1 or more" clause is gone too; read literally, a fighter with
  no armor at all would roll whenever something pulls their Endure Threshold under 10 (ruling 74
  keeps the old gate).
- Sight and hearing lost its last sentence, and Speed at the margin lost its last sentence. The
  first sentence of each still carries the rule.
- The plate example is now two paragraphs, with the same numbers (Load 8, Load Strain 7, 6 on a
  fighter Trained in Endure, 4 at Master). The Trained-versus-Expert Wind sentence the proposal
  added was not kept.
- Conditions table, Fatigued N: "−N Condition (maximum of 3) penalty to Evade, Guard, and Attack
  rolls; can't use Exploration Mode Activities. Ends after ten minutes of rest."
- Berserker, Rage (the Root Talent's Effect), the two sentences that changed: "Afterward, increase
  your Fatigued by 1 until you spend three actions to catch your breath." and "You may end your
  Rage as a free action, increasing your Fatigued by 1 as though it had run its course." Everything
  else in the cell is unchanged, and so is Deaf to Pain: "You ignore the Fatigued condition while
  Raging, and when your Rage ends you need to spend only 1 action to catch your breath rather than
  3."

## Rulings

74. **No Wind check for a fighter whose Endure Threshold is at least 10 + Load Strain; and the
    inverted clause.** Mike's saved sentence read "If 10 + your Load Strain is less than your
    Endure Threshold, then at the end of the third round ... you must roll Endure against 10 + Load
    Strain", which would make the fit fighter roll and spare the winded one. The intended reading
    is the one his accepted proposal carried, and the one his own note asked for ("should not have
    to roll to be Winded"), so the edition turns the comparison round: no Wind check when the
    fighter's Endure Threshold is at least 10 + Load Strain; otherwise the check as 0.6.0 ran it,
    from the end of the third round, every round, a failure raising Fatigued by 1 to the ceiling of
    3. The correction is the `phb_edit.py` entry that writes v4.12; Mike is asked below to confirm
    it. Two readings inside the rule. First, **the Endure Threshold is the one the Defense grid
    shows**: 10 + Might bonus + Endure Proficiency Bonus (Endure feeds Might), with whatever the
    Defense path folds in, so Frightened N lowers it and Fatigued N does not (Fatigued touches
    Evade, Guard and attack rolls only). It is read live, as every other derived number is, so a
    Frightened fighter can lose the exemption for a round and get it back. Second, **the "Load
    Strain 1 or more" gate stays**: the v4.12 sentence has dropped it, and for an unpenalised
    fighter it is implied (at Strain 0 the Wind Threshold is 10 and an Endure Threshold starts at
    10), but Frightened N, or a negative Endure adjustment, can pull an Endure Threshold under 10,
    and the sentence read literally would then make an unarmored fighter roll for Wind. Nothing is
    winding them, so the system keeps the gate: no Load Strain, no check. A clause in the book
    would settle it ("a fighter carrying Load Strain whose Endure Threshold ...").
    System: the data model derives `wind` (`threshold` = 10 + Load Strain, `endureThreshold`,
    `exempt`) once Load Strain and the Defenses are both known (`data/actor.mjs`); the round-end
    code posts no Wind card for an exempt fighter, falling back to the comparison when the field is
    missing, as on an adversary (`documents/combat.mjs`, `endRound`); a card already posted still
    rolls (`onWindCheck`); the Load Strain field carries a tooltip for every viewer, GM or player,
    stating the Wind Threshold and whether the character is exempt, with the numbers
    (`apps/actor-sheet.mjs`, `templates/actor/overview.hbs`; the locked-field behaviour is kept).
    Web app: the sheet's Strain line reads "no Wind check (Endure Threshold N meets M)" when exempt
    and "Wind from round 3: Endure vs M" otherwise, and the wiki states the rule in the book's terms.
    The printed sheet's Defense ladder line (`sheet_spec.json`, `defense.ladder_micro`) notes the
    exemption in as few words as fit its two lines.
75. **The Fatigued row.** The Conditions table is now the one definition of Fatigued N, and it says
    "Ends after ten minutes of rest" with nothing about the fight being over. The roster's
    `conditions` row (`assets/roster.json`) and the system's Fatigued hint, Wind hint and Wind card
    texts (`lang/en.json`) carry the new wording; nothing downstream says "once the fight is over"
    any more. The mechanics are unchanged from ruling 69: Fatigued N is a value-carrying condition,
    −N Condition to Evade, Guard and attack rolls, raised one step at a time by a failed Wind check,
    to 3. Ruling 70 cleared it when the Combat document is deleted as the nearest thing the system
    can see to "ten minutes of rest once the fight is over"; the book no longer ties the rest to the
    fight, so that clear is kept as an approximation of the ten minutes rather than as the rule's
    letter, and the question below asks whether a clear from the rest card should replace it or
    join it.
76. **Rage takes the book's sentences verbatim.** The Rage Talent's Effect on the Berserker sheet
    of `data/callings.xlsx` (cell G2, rich text; the bold runs are untouched) replaces "Afterward,
    you're fatigued until you spend three actions to catch your breath." and "You may end your Rage
    as a free action, becoming fatigued as though it had run its course." with the two v4.12
    sentences. Deaf to Pain (G6) is unchanged. Rage is not automated, so nothing in the system acts
    on the text and no code moved; the Constellation Compendium and the web app pick it up from
    `trees.json`. The v4.11 report's ruling 73, which kept the old wording as the Talent's own
    sense of the word, is closed by this: Rage's aftermath is now the Wind condition's Fatigued,
    raised by 1, and Deaf to Pain's "ignore the Fatigued condition while Raging" reads against the
    same condition. If Rage's aftermath should one day set Fatigued on the sheet, that is a
    Talent-automation question for the Automation column, as before.
77. **Endure ships, with its Root alone.** Mike set Endure Training's `Enabled?` to Yes on the
    Endure sheet of `data/defenses.xlsx`; Shrug It Off, Second Wind ❷, Braced Frame and Blade
    Brake ❶↺ stay blank. A Constellation ships when its Root does (ruling 60), so the Endure
    Constellation and its one Talent go into the compendia, Endure joins Evade and Guard as a
    Defense the wizard's Defenses step can Train (Awareness Training is still blank, so Awareness
    waits), and the two things Endure Training does in play, the relief that lowers Load Strain and
    the Endure Bonus that lifts Vigor from Expert, are reachable for the first time. The enabled
    counts become 12 of 31 Constellations and 38 of 177 Talents; `content/sync.json` carries them
    once the integrator's build runs, and the Foundry README and `FEATURES.md` say so. No code
    moved: the gate was already data.
78. **The trimmed sentences are flavour.** "The Head Zone's Protection is bought with your eyes"
    (Sight and hearing) and "Nothing else does: a grapple, a tumble, and a single Move at full
    Speed are yours at full strength" (Speed at the margin) are gone from the book. Neither carried
    a rule its bullet's first sentence does not, so nothing downstream changes beyond no longer
    quoting them; the 0.6.0 changelog entry keeps its quotation as the record of what v4.11 said.
    Explanatory text that says no other check takes Load Strain remains true and stays.

## Questions for the author

- **Confirm the corrected Wind clause.** The working file saved "If 10 + your Load Strain is less
  than your Endure Threshold, then ... you must roll Endure"; the v4.12 edition prints "If your
  Endure Threshold is less than 10 + your Load Strain, then ... you must roll Endure". The second
  is the proposal's reading and the one the system implements: the fighter whose Endure Threshold
  already meets the Wind Threshold is the one who never rolls. If the first was meant, the system
  follows it in one comparison, but the rule would then spare the fighter most likely to fail.
- **Is the exemption meant to be read live?** Ruling 74 reads it from the Endure Threshold as the
  grid shows it at the end of the round, Frightened included, so a fighter who is exempt at rest
  can be made to roll by fear. If the intent is a standing property of the build (the Threshold
  with no conditions on it), the derivation drops the Condition stack and the sentence in the book
  might say "your Endure Threshold, before penalties".
- **Does a fighter with no Load Strain ever roll for Wind?** The v4.12 sentence dropped "a fighter
  with Load Strain 1 or more", and read literally it would make an unarmored fighter roll whenever
  Frightened pulls their Endure Threshold under 10. Ruling 74 keeps the old gate (no Load Strain, no
  check), since nothing is winding them. One clause in the book settles it either way: "a fighter
  carrying Load Strain whose Endure Threshold is less than 10 + Load Strain".
- **Should Fatigued outlive the Combat now?** v4.11 said "ten minutes of rest once the fight is
  over"; v4.12 says "Ends after ten minutes of rest". The system still clears Fatigued when the
  Combat document is deleted (ruling 70), which was a fair reading of the first and is only an
  approximation of the second. The alternatives: clear it from the rest card as well (ten minutes
  is less than a night, so the night's rest certainly ends it), or from the rest card instead (so
  a fight that ends and a chase that begins in the same scene keeps the Fatigued until someone
  rests). A word from Mike picks one; `FEATURES.md` names the current behaviour as an
  approximation either way.
- **Were the two trimmed sentences intentional?** Sight and hearing lost "The Head Zone's
  Protection is bought with your eyes" and Speed at the margin lost "Nothing else does: a grapple,
  a tumble, and a single Move at full Speed are yours at full strength". Both read as deliberate
  tightening, and nothing downstream leaned on them; this is only a check that they did not go
  with the tracked changes by accident.
- The v4.10 report's questions stand in v4.12, each checked against the edition: Key Terms says
  Attribute Bonus is points "divided by 3", that a Defense is one of four Constellations "every
  character begins play Trained in", and names Varisians; Drilled says "Weapons Proficiency
  Bonus"; Table 9's Quick row says a Miss costs "Nothing"; Melee Training grants Counter beside
  Intercept; Aid is "Aid ❶ (⓿↺)" in the book and a single action in the sheet; the Example of Play
  still sums at d20+6; Armored Fighting is "Combat Style • Presence" in its heading and Might in
  the Combat Styles table.
