# Traits, Actions & Activities

Draft copy for Chapter 2. Traits come first because every action entry uses them.

---

# Part 1: Traits

> A **trait** is a word attached to an action, a talent, a weapon, or a piece of gear that plugs it
> into a rule somewhere else. Traits save the game from repeating itself: rather than writing "this
> counts toward your multiple attack penalty" on forty different things, each one simply has the
> **attack** trait.
>
> Traits are never flavour. If a word appears in a trait line, some rule cares about it.

### Action traits

| Trait | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Counts toward your multiple attack penalty, and suffers it. Your first attack in a turn is unpenalized, your second takes −5, and your third and beyond take −10. |
| **Move** | You change position under your own power. Moving out of a foe's reach can provoke a Reactive Strike from anyone who has one. |
| **Manipulate** | You use your hands. You need at least one free, and fine work near an enemy can provoke. |
| **Concentrate** | You need a clear head. Some effects, and enough damage, can break it. |
| **Flourish** | A signature effort. You can use only one flourish action per turn. |
| **Stance** | A way of standing and holding yourself. You can be in only one stance at a time, and it lasts until you leave it, fall unconscious, or enter another. |
| **Open** | Only usable as the very first action of your turn, before you have attacked. |
| **Auditory** | It works through sound. It fails on the deafened, and on anyone who cannot hear you. |
| **Visual** | It works through sight. It fails on the blinded, and on anyone who cannot see you. |
| **Mental** | It works on the mind. It fails on anything mindless. |
| **Emotion** | A mental effect that works on feeling. Anything immune to emotion ignores it. |
| **Reaction** | Not a trait so much as a cost: it happens on someone else's turn, and you have one per round. |

**On Reactive Strike.** Nobody has one by default. It is bought in a Combat Style, which is why the
**move** trait matters more against some opponents than others. Ask before you assume the enemy has
teeth.

**A note on secret rolls.** This game has none. The GM sets Thresholds and may keep them hidden, but
the die is always in a player's hand. If you are searching for something you might not find, you
roll, and the GM tells you what you learn rather than whether you succeeded.

### Weapon and armor traits

These arrive in Chapter 4 with the gear that carries them. A few are worth knowing early because the
actions below reference them:

| Trait | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Agile** | Your multiple attack penalty with this weapon is −4 and −8 rather than −5 and −10. |
| **Finesse** | You may use Agility instead of Might on attacks with it. Damage still uses Might. |
| **Reach** | It threatens at 10 feet rather than 5. |
| **Trip**, **Shove**, **Disarm** | You may attempt that maneuver with this weapon in hand rather than a free hand. |

# Part 2: Actions & Activities

> An **action** takes one of the three you get each turn, marked ◆. An **activity** takes two or
> three, marked ◆◆ or ◆◆◆, and is a single indivisible thing: interrupt it and you lose all of it. A
> **free action** ◇ costs nothing but can only be taken when its rule allows. A **reaction** ↺
> happens on someone else's turn, and you get one per round.
>
> Every entry below lists its traits. If an action calls for a roll, the Threshold is named.

## Moving

**Stride ◆** *(move)*
Move up to your Speed. You may break it up around other actions, but each Stride is its own action.

**Step ◆**
Move 5 feet. Step has no traits at all, which is the point: it never provokes. You cannot Step into
difficult terrain.

**Leap ◆** *(move)*
Jump 10 feet horizontally or 3 feet vertically, without a check. Longer or higher jumps are an
Athletics check, and count as part of the Leap.

**Crawl ◆** *(move)*
While prone, move 5 feet.

**Stand ◆** *(move)*
Get up from prone.

**Climb ◆** or **Swim ◆** *(move)*
An Athletics check against a Threshold the GM sets. On a success you move a quarter of your Speed
(half on a critical success). On a critical failure you fall, or sink.

**Tumble Through ◆** *(move)*
Acrobatics against the target's Evade or Guard, their choice, to move through a foe's space. On a
success you pass through and their space is not difficult terrain for this move. On a failure your
movement stops in front of them.

## Attacking

**Strike ◆** *(attack)*
Attack with a weapon or an unarmed blow. Roll **Weapons** against the target's chosen Defense. Your
combat style sets which attribute you add and opens that style's exploits, but the proficiency is
always Weapons.

**Grapple ◆** *(attack)*
Athletics against the target's Evade or Guard, their choice. You need a free hand or a weapon with
the grapple trait, and the target must be within reach and no more than one size larger than you.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical hit | The target is **restrained** until the end of your next turn. |
| Hit | The target is **grabbed** until the end of your next turn. |
| Graze | No effect, but the target still pays the Graze cost. |
| Miss | You overcommit and are **off-guard** until your next turn. |

**Shove ◆** *(attack)*
Athletics against the target's Evade or Guard. You need a free hand or a shove-trait weapon.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical hit | Push the target 10 feet. You may Stride after it, keeping it in reach. |
| Hit | Push the target 5 feet. |
| Graze | No effect, but the target still pays the Graze cost. |
| Miss | You overcommit and are off-guard until your next turn. |

**Trip ◆** *(attack)*
Athletics against the target's Evade or Guard. You need a free hand or a trip-trait weapon, and the
target must be no more than one size larger.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical hit | The target falls **prone** and takes damage equal to your Might. |
| Hit | The target falls **prone**. |
| Graze | No effect, but the target still pays the Graze cost. |
| Miss | You fall prone yourself. |

**Disarm ◆** *(attack)*
Athletics against the target's Evade or Guard. You need a free hand or a disarm-trait weapon.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical hit | The item flies from their grip and lands within 10 feet. |
| Hit | Their grip is loosened: they take a −2 circumstance penalty to attacks with it, and the next Disarm against them gains +2, until they spend an Interact to resettle it. |
| Graze | No effect, but the target still pays the Graze cost. |
| Miss | You are off-guard until your next turn. |

**Escape ◆** *(attack)*
Break free of a grab, a restraint, or anything holding you. Roll your best of Athletics, Acrobatics,
or Endure against the grabber's Evade or Guard. On a success the condition ends. On a critical
success it ends and you may Step.

> **The Graze band on a maneuver is where a shield press is won.** A Shove that Grazes moves nobody
> and still drives the defender's guard out of line, or takes their ground. Grinding at someone
> works even when nothing visible happens.

## Defending and recovering

**Raise a Shield ◆**
Gain your shield's circumstance bonus to Guard until the start of your next turn. You must be
wielding it.

**Recenter ◆**
Gather yourself: clear every Exposed zone on you and end Pressed.

**Take Cover ◆**
If you are near something to get behind, gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Evade until you move or
attack. Behind a wall or a doorway, +4.

**Ready ◆◆** *(concentrate)*
Name a single action and a trigger for it. Until the start of your next turn, if the trigger occurs
you may take that action as a reaction. Readying consumes your reaction whether or not it fires.

**Delay ◇**
Taken at the start of your turn, before anything else: give up the rest of your turn and re-enter the
order later in the round at any point you choose. Your reaction is lost until you act.

## Watching and deceiving

**Seek ◆** *(concentrate)*
Awareness against the Stealth of anything hiding, or against a Threshold the GM sets for a hidden
object. Covers everything within 30 feet.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Critical success | You find it, precisely, and it is no longer Hidden or Undetected to you. |
| Success | It becomes Concealed to you rather than Hidden, or Hidden rather than Undetected. |
| Failure | You learn nothing. |
| Critical failure | You are certain of something wrong, and the GM will tell you what. |

**Hide ◆** *(secret to nobody; you roll)*
Stealth against the Awareness of each creature you are trying to escape. You need cover or
concealment. On a success you are **Hidden** to those you beat.

**Sneak ◆** *(move)*
Move up to half your Speed while staying Hidden. Roll Stealth against the Awareness of anyone who
might notice. On a failure you are found at the end of the movement.

**Point Out ◆** *(auditory, visual)*
Indicate a creature you can see to your allies. It becomes Hidden rather than Undetected to any ally
who can hear and see you.

**Demoralize ◆** *(auditory, concentrate, emotion, mental)*
Intimidation against the target's Guard. On a success it is **frightened 1**, on a critical success
**frightened 2**. You cannot Demoralize the same creature again for a minute.

**Feint ◆** *(mental)*
Deception against the target's Awareness. On a success it is **off-guard** to your next melee attack
this turn; on a critical success, off-guard to all your melee attacks until the end of your turn. On
a critical failure you are off-guard to it instead.

**Aid ↺**
You must have prepared to help during your own last turn, naming the ally and the task. When they
attempt it, roll a relevant check against a Threshold the GM sets. On a success they gain a +1
circumstance bonus, on a critical success +2. On a critical failure they take −1.

**Recall Knowledge ◆** *(concentrate)*
A Lore or Skill check against a Threshold the GM sets. Learn something true about a creature, place,
or object.

## Handling things

**Interact ◆** *(manipulate)*
Draw or stow a weapon, open a door, pull a potion from your pack, pick something up, work a lever.
One sensible manipulation.

**Release ◇**
Let go of something you are holding. Free, and always available.

**Drop Prone ◇**
Fall prone. Free. Standing up again is not.

# Part 3: Concealed, Hidden, and Undetected

> Being hard to find is a ladder with three rungs, and **Awareness climbs down it.** Every rung is
> answered by Seek, and no rung uses a flat check.

| State | What your enemy knows | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Concealed** | Exactly where you are, but not clearly | You gain a **+2 circumstance bonus to Evade**. |
| **Hidden** | Roughly where you are | You gain a **+4 circumstance bonus to Evade**, and attacks against you must guess your square. |
| **Undetected** | Nothing | You cannot be targeted at all. An enemy may attack the square they last believed you occupied. |

> **Seek ◆** moves a creature one rung back toward you: Undetected becomes Hidden, Hidden becomes
> Concealed, Concealed becomes plainly seen. A critical success skips a rung.
>
> **A creature that cannot see you is off-guard to you.** That is what being unseen buys on offense,
> and it is why the Ambusher wants the dark.
>
> Fog, dim light, and a blur make you Concealed without hiding you. **Hide ◆** and **Sneak ◆** are how
> you climb the ladder deliberately, and both are Stealth against the Awareness of whoever might spot
> you.

## Notes on the drafting

**Concealed and Hidden are now Evade bonuses rather than flat checks**, which does three things: it
puts Awareness on both sides of the exchange, it removes the last flat checks from combat, and it
keeps the die in the defender's hand where player-facing rolls want it. The old DC 5 flat check
turned about 20% of hits into misses; +2 Evade turns about 10% of them. If concealment feels too weak
in play, the dial is +3 and +5.

**Every maneuver's Graze band is "no effect, but the defender still pays the Graze cost."** That is
consistent across all four and worth stating once in the rules rather than four times in the tables.

**Escape now accepts Endure** alongside Athletics and Acrobatics, which gives Endure one more
frequent job and fits "the body that will not be held."

**Two open questions.** Whether Ready should cost two actions (pf2e) or one, given your three-action
round already feels tight. And whether Aid's Threshold should be a flat 15, a level-based Threshold,
or the GM's call; I have written it as the GM's call, since the flat 15 stopped scaling by level 5.
