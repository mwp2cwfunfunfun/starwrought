# v4.11 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.10 to v4.11, synced 2026-10-02. A point release of the rules, not a rewrite.
Mike accepted two proposals made with `assets/phb_propose.py` (the vigor proposal and the
armor-balance proposal) with tweaks of his own, and the book moved in two places. Vigor is an
Opening sum from the first Calling plus a per-level share that grows with Endure, and Load Strain
comes off the margins of a fight (a Rush, a Leap, a Climb, a Swim, a Stealth check, and breath as
the rounds run on) rather than off Evade. Everything downstream moved: the Endure sheet of
`data/defenses.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`, `assets/sheet_spec.json`, the web app, the character
sheets, the Constellation Compendium, the Foundry
system (0.6.0), its `FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and `data/README.txt`.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook leaves something to the implementation, and
the question Mike raised while accepting the changes. The numbering continues the v4.10 report's,
which ended at 66; each ruling also carries the R label the sync brief gave it, so the brief, the
changelog and this report name the same thing the same way.

## What the book now says

Quoted exactly, because every ruling below interprets one of these sentences.

- Chapter 2, Your Vigor: "At 1st level: Your Ancestry's Vigor + Endure Bonus + your first
  Calling's Opening Vigor + 10". "At every level thereafter: Your Ancestry's Vigor + Endure Bonus."
  "A full night's rest restores Vigor equal to your level * your Presence (if positive)."
- Chapter 3: "Each Calling grants Training in 1 Skill or other Constellation, gives you a special
  ability, and adds Vigor (if it is your first Calling)." The Callings table's column is "Opening
  Vigor": Berserker 12, Ambusher 8, Hunter 10, Bravo 10, Weaponmaster 10 (it was "Vigor/lvl" 4, 2,
  3, 3, 3). The level-up list says "+Vigor equal to Ancestry Vigor + Endure Bonus". The Ancestry
  table keeps "Vigor/lvl" (Human 8).
- Endure Training, the Endure root, new tail: "You also reduce your Load Strain by 1 (to a minimum
  of 0) at Trained, 2 at Expert, 3 at Master, and 4 at Legendary. Your Vigor grows with your
  conditioning as well: at Expert rank in Endure you gain 1 more Vigor at every level, 2 at Master,
  and 3 at Legendary."
- Chapter 5, Load and Load Strain: "Load Strain = the Load of all the armor you are wearing and of
  a shield you carry, less 1 for a matched harness and less your Endure relief (both below), to a
  minimum of 0. It never comes off your Evade, and it is no blanket penalty on Might or Agility: a
  fit fighter in a fitted harness runs, rolls, and wrestles like anyone else. What it costs is
  breath, senses, and the margins of movement, as the three points below say." "Armor does not
  make you easier to hit, and it costs a fighter nothing on the roll, Guard or Evade." Wind: "At
  the end of the third round of an encounter and every round after, a fighter with Load Strain 1
  or more rolls Endure against 10 + Load Strain. On a failure they are Fatigued 1, or their
  Fatigued rises by 1, to a maximum of 3. Fatigued N is a −N Condition penalty to Evade, Guard,
  and attack rolls, and it lasts until ten minutes of rest once the fight is over." "Your Rush and
  Leap distances are reduced by your Load Strain in feet, and Climb, Swim, and Stealth checks take
  your Load Strain as a penalty. Nothing else does: a grapple, a tumble, and a single Move at full
  Speed are yours at full strength." "Training in Endure reduces Load Strain as your Proficiency
  Rank in it rises: by 1 at Trained, 2 at Expert, 3 at Master, and 4 at Legendary." The worked
  example: full plate with closed helm is "Load 8, Load Strain 7 (less 1 for the matched harness,
  and less your Endure relief: 6 on a fighter Trained in Endure, 4 at Master)". Sleeping in armor:
  "You cannot sleep in armor without waking Fatigued 1 unless your torso piece has comfort."
- Key Terms, Load: "it never makes you easier to hit, but it shortens a Rush and a Leap, weighs on
  a Climb, a Swim, and a Stealth check, and it tires you as a fight runs long".
- Conditions table: "Fatigued N | −N Condition penalty to Evade, Guard, and attack rolls; can't use
  Exploration Mode Activities. Rises by 1 on each failed Wind check, to a maximum of 3; ends after
  ten minutes of rest once the fight is over."

## Rulings

67. **(R1) Vigor is 10 + Opening Vigor + (Ancestry Vigor + Endure Bonus) × level.** Three readings
    inside one formula. First, **"Endure Bonus" is the conditioning number in Endure Training**, 0
    at Untrained and Trained, 1 at Expert, 2 at Master, 3 at Legendary. The book never defines the
    term; it is neither Endure's Attribute Bonus nor its Proficiency Bonus, since the only
    Endure-flavoured number the book says you "gain at every level" is the conditioning clause, and
    reading it as Proficiency would hand a Trained character 3 Vigor a level the Talent never
    mentions. Second, **it is read live from the actor's current Endure rank and multiplied by
    level**, so "1 more Vigor at every level" is taken literally: reaching Expert lifts every
    level's share, the levels already behind you included, which is how every other derived number
    in the system recomputes from the present state (Attribute Bonus, rank, Protection) rather than
    from a history. It counts at 1st level too, where it is always 0 because Expert needs 5th. If
    Mike means only the levels gained after the rank is reached, the system would have to record
    the level each rank arrived at, which it does not today; see the questions below. Third, **the
    Opening Vigor is paid once, by the first Calling**, and the Ancestry's number is per level, as
    the two tables' headings say. So: Vigor max = 10 + Opening Vigor + (Ancestry Vigor + Endure
    Bonus) × level, plus any `bonuses.vigor` the system already adds. A 1st-level Human Weaponmaster
    has 28 (10 + 10 + 8 × 1); the same character at 5th with Expert Endure has 65 (10 + 10 +
    (8 + 1) × 5); Mira of the Long Road, a Human Ambusher, goes from 20 to 26. A night's rest is
    unchanged: level × Presence bonus when that bonus is positive, else level, today's reading of
    "(if positive)". The system exposes `vigor.perLevel` (Ancestry + Endure Bonus) and
    `vigor.opening` for the sheet and the tooltips (`data/actor.mjs`, `#prepareVigor`); the chargen
    review prints the formula with the character's numbers in it (`apps/chargen.mjs`); the
    locked-field tooltips say the Calling's number is paid once and the Ancestry's every level
    (`apps/actor-sheet.mjs`, `lockedTips`); the chassis Item sheet labels a Calling's number
    "Opening Vigor" and an Ancestry's "Vigor per level" (`templates/item/details.hbs`).
68. **(R2) Load Strain never modifies Evade; Climb, Swim and Stealth take it; nothing else does.**
    "It never comes off your Evade" and "Armor does not make you easier to hit" are the book's own
    words, so the untyped Strain modifier is gone from the Evade modifier list and from the Evade
    Threshold (`data/actor.mjs`); the old blanket penalty on Might and Agility Skill checks goes
    with it, since "it is no blanket penalty on Might or Agility". Rush and Leap keep their Strain
    in feet (ruling 7 stands). Of the three checks the book names, **Stealth is a Constellation**,
    so a Stealth check carries the penalty automatically, untyped and labelled Load Strain on the
    card (`dice/check.mjs`, the base assembly). **Climb and Swim are uses of Athletics the system
    cannot tell from a grapple**, so the roll dialog for an Athletics check offers an unticked
    "Load Strain (Climb or Swim)" modifier whenever the actor's Strain is 1 or more, and the player
    ticks it when the check is one of those (`dice/check.mjs`, the dialog's optional modifiers and
    `answer.extra`). That is the working agreement's standing rule applied: a judgement the engine
    cannot make is put at the edge, as a question, rather than guessed in the middle. The penalty
    stays untyped, as Load Strain always has been (untyped is for the system's own flat
    adjustments; no Talent bonus is untyped). No other check takes Strain. If Climb and Swim ever
    become Maneuvers of their own in `data/maneuvers.xlsx`, the toggle can go and the penalty can
    ride on them.
69. **(R3) Fatigued is Fatigued N, 1 to 3, and the Wind check raises it.** "Fatigued 1, or their
    Fatigued rises by 1, to a maximum of 3" makes Fatigued a value-carrying condition like
    Frightened N, so it is stored and shown the same way (`setCondition(key, value)`, the palette,
    the effect's value; the sheet's Effects tab and the palette read "Fatigued N"). It applies as a
    −N Condition penalty to Evade, Guard and attack rolls, melee and ranged (`data/actor.mjs` for
    the Defenses, `dice/check.mjs` kind "attack"), in place of the boolean −1 to Evade and Guard
    the system carried under v4.10 (`FATIGUED_PENALTY`). It is a Condition penalty, so it does not
    stack with Frightened N (ruling 36 stands): the worse of the two applies. The Wind reminder
    posts at the end of every round from the third for anyone with Load Strain 1 or more who is
    not Fatigued 3, unconscious or Dying (it used to stop once Fatigued at all, there being nowhere
    further to go); a failed check sets Fatigued 1 or raises it by 1 to 3, and the card says the new
    value (`documents/combat.mjs`, `onWindCheck`; `STARWROUGHT.Combat.windText`, `windFatigued`,
    `windNote` carry the v4.11 wording). "Can't use Exploration Mode Activities" is the table's to
    hold, like every other Activity gate. **Sleeping in armor without Comfort ("waking Fatigued
    1") is not built**: the rest card restores Vigor and nothing else, it never implemented the
    v4.10 version of the rule, and the brief said to add nothing.
70. **(R4) Fatigued clears when the encounter ends.** "It lasts until ten minutes of rest once the
    fight is over." The system has no clock for ten minutes of rest and no notion of the fight
    being over except the Combat document being deleted, so that deletion is what clears Fatigued,
    beside the end-of-combat hooks that already clear remembered targets (`canvas/targeting.mjs`)
    and Intercept charges (`documents/actions.mjs`), on the client the neighbours use. No clear
    existed before: a boolean Fatigued stayed until someone toggled it off, so this is new
    behaviour rather than a retargeted trigger. Two consequences, both for the table: a fight that
    ends and a chase that begins inside the same Combat keeps the Fatigued, and a GM who deletes
    the Combat but wants the Fatigued to linger into the next scene sets it again from the
    palette. FEATURES.md names this as an approximation.
71. **(R5) Endure relief is Trained 1, Expert 2, Master 3, Legendary 4; Untrained 0.** The book
    says it twice, in Endure Training and in Chapter 5, and the worked example does the sum (plate
    at Strain 7 before relief, 6 Trained, 4 Master). `LOAD_RELIEF` in `config.mjs` reads so; it had
    read Expert 1, Master 2, Legendary 3 since system 0.2.0, following the v3.2 reword of Endure
    Training. This amends ruling 34 of the v4.10 report, which upheld that ladder and "corrected"
    the web app's table away from Trained 1 / Expert 2 / Master 3 / Legendary 4: the table the app
    had carried by mistake since v3.2 is the one the book now prints, so the app reads it again,
    this time on purpose. The Endure Training Effect cell on the Endure sheet of
    `data/defenses.xlsx` carries the book's tail, relief and Vigor clause both (the sync brief
    named a `data/endure.xlsx` that does not exist; the four Defenses share one workbook).
72. **(R6) Existing characters' Calling Vigor migrates to Opening Vigor when it still holds the
    book's old number.** `system.details.calling.vigor` held the per-level number. The 0.6.0 world
    migration (`starwrought.mjs`, `migrateWorld`, added to the `needs(...)` list and run once by the
    first GM to open the world, recorded in `systemVersion` as the 0.4.0 Speed conversion and the
    0.5.1 aura step were) sets it to the Calling's Opening Vigor when the stored value equals the
    old table value for the named Calling (Berserker 4 to 12, Ambusher 2 to 8, Hunter 3 to 10,
    Bravo 3 to 10, Weaponmaster 3 to 10), read from the shipped chassis index
    (`content/chassis.json`, `system.vigor` of the Calling) or from that old-to-new table, and
    leaves any other value alone, since a number that is not the book's is one the GM typed.
    `prepareDerivedData` then clamps `vigor.value` to the new maximum, so a re-render suffices. A
    chat or console line says how many characters it touched, as the 0.5.1 step does. The reading
    behind the rule: equality with the old table is the only evidence the system has that a value
    is the book's rather than the GM's, and a GM who typed the book's number by hand loses nothing
    by having it moved.
73. **(R7) Rage's "fatigued" stays as the sheet has it.** The Rage Talent in `callings.xlsx` says
    "Afterward, you're fatigued until you spend three actions to catch your breath", and another
    Berserker Talent says you "ignore the Fatigued condition while Raging". Both use the word in
    the Talent's own sense, the aftermath of a Rage; nothing in the system acts on either, the
    cells are Mike's, and the system's Fatigued N is the Wind condition. If Rage's aftermath should
    one day set Fatigued 1, that is a Talent-automation question for the Automation column, not a
    rename.

## Questions for the author

- **A threshold past which no Wind check is rolled** (Mike, 2026-10-02, accepting the Load Strain
  changes: "If the character passes a certain threshold something related, I think they should not
  have to roll to be Winded. I will eventually have an Armored Fighting Constellation too, which
  might include some talents related to this."). Nothing is built; today every fighter with Load
  Strain 1 or more rolls from the end of the third round, and bringing Strain to 0 through Endure
  relief is the only way out of the check. The round-end test in `documents/combat.mjs` is the one
  place a gate would go, and each of these shapes is one condition there:
  - **A rank in Endure.** No Wind check at Master (or Expert) in Endure. Cheap, and it rewards the
    Constellation the rule already leans on; it also makes Endure relief and the exemption two
    gifts from one root.
  - **A Strain ceiling.** No Wind check at Load Strain N or less (2, say): a light harness is worn
    all day, a full one tires you. This is the reading closest to "passes a certain threshold" and
    the easiest for a player to see on the sheet.
  - **A Threshold the fighter cannot fail.** No roll when Endure's modifier already meets 10 +
    Strain less 1, so only a natural 1 could fail. It saves a die that is nearly always a formality,
    but it also removes a roll that could have Flared, and a critical failure on a Wind check is one
    of Endure's few Flares in a fight.
  - **A Talent flag**, in the Armored Fighting Constellation Mike means to fill out. The right home
    for a conditioning Talent ("Second Wind", "Bred to the Harness"), and the Automation column is
    where it would be authored once that column has a grammar; until then it would be a hard-coded
    Talent name, which the working agreement discourages.
  Whichever Mike chooses, the sentence belongs in the Wind paragraph of Chapter 5 and the system
  follows it in one line.
- **Is the Endure Bonus retroactive?** Ruling 67 reads "1 more Vigor at every level" as every
  level, including those already gained, because the system derives from the present state and
  keeps no history of when a rank arrived. If the intent is only the levels gained after reaching
  Expert, the book needs a sentence saying so and the system a stored level per rank.
- **Should Fatigued survive the end of the Combat document?** Ruling 70 clears it on deletion,
  which is the nearest thing the system can see to "ten minutes of rest once the fight is over".
  If Fatigued is meant to carry into a chase or a second wave, the clear should move to the rest
  card instead, and FEATURES.md would say so.
- **Sleeping in armor without Comfort** gives Fatigued 1 in the book and nothing in the system;
  the rest card could set it if the armor worn is read at rest. Not built, per the brief.
- The v4.10 report's questions stand. v4.11 still carries them all: Key Terms says ÷ 3, four
  Trained Defenses and Varisians; Drilled says "Weapons Proficiency Bonus"; Table 9's Quick row
  says "Nothing"; Melee Training grants Counter; Aid differs between sheet and book; Armored
  Fighting's Key Attribute is Presence in its heading and Might in the table.
