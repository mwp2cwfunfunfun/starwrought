## 2. Rules the app now implements

- **AC is gone.** Attacks are rolled against a Defense *Threshold* (10 + level + attribute +
  proficiency). Defenders roll too: beat by 10 = Miss, meet = Graze, miss = Hit, miss by 10 = crit.
- **Four Defenses**, all Trained free at creation: Awareness (Wits), Evade (Agility), Guard
  (Presence), Endure (Might).
- **Zone armor.** Head / Torso / Arms / Legs, each with its own Protection and Load. Matched harness
  (+1 torso, −1 Load) and Clatter are both implemented. Load Strain = armor Load − Might − Endure's
  reduction, minimum 0, subtracted from Evade.
- **Graze costs**: Evade yields 5 ft or takes an Exposed zone; Guard holds and becomes Pressed.
- **Size**: Small +1 Evade / −1 Guard; Large the reverse.
- **HP = 10 + (ancestry HP + calling HP) × level.** Might no longer contributes.
- **Damage = weapon dice + specialization only.** Might no longer adds to damage. Dice scale
  1 / 2 / 3 / 4 at levels 1 / 4 / 12 / 19.
- **Chargen is 8 points, not 12.** Ancestry, heritage, and culture roots are free and buy nothing;
  background gives 2 (one per named skill); Calling gives 1 plus a Training in the skill it teaches;
  Defenses give 1; Comets give 3.
- Saved characters from v1.1 are migrated on load: Fortitude→Endure, Reflex→Evade, Will→Guard,
  Flares moved with them, renamed talents mapped, retired trees dropped.

## 3. Places the PHB contradicts itself, and how I ruled

These are the calls that could go the other way. Each one is a one-line change if you disagree.

1. **Shield Fighting's attribute.** The Combat Styles summary table (ch. 6) says **Might**. The
   Shield Fighting section subtitle says **Presence**, and so does the Key Attribute column on both
   Shield Fighting Training and Shield Block. I went with **Presence**: two of three mentions, and
   shields feed Guard, which is a Presence defense. `data/combat_styles.xlsx` → `_Tree Index` → Feeds.

2. **Rage's duration.** The talent says "Duration 10 rounds" and then "for the encounter" in the same
   block. Both are printed verbatim in the sheet. Needs one of them struck.

3. **The Lore root dropped its proficiency clause.** Every other skill root reads "add the
   constellation's rank to its checks"; Lore's v1.8 text does not. Since ch. 2 makes proficiency
   universal, I **restored the clause**. Without it the root also violates the Root Rule.

4. **Attribute bonus minimum.** `CLAUDE.md` says "min +1". The PHB's own table says 0–2 points = +0.
   I followed **the PHB**, so a single point in a constellation now grants +0 rather than +1. This is
   a real nerf to one-point dips and worth a deliberate look.

5. **"Opening Talent Point"** (Versatile Human) is never defined anywhere in the PHB, and the Comets
   section (ch. 3) is an **empty heading**. I read it as "1 point that opens any constellation you
   haven't started" with no non-Skill restriction, since v1.8 dropped the old wording. If it should
   stay non-Skill, change the Grants cell to `1 open non-Skill`.

6. **Dueling Training** read "You gain the The Measure action". Fixed as a typo.

## 4. Root Rule violations the PHB introduces

The pipeline now warns on four roots. All four are PHB text as written, so I did not "fix" them:

```
Human / Versatile Human   nothing rolled + no rank scaling   ("You gain 1 Opening Talent Point.")
Human / Torchbearer Human no rank scaling
Keleshite / Keleshite     nothing rolled + no rank scaling   ("You learn the Kelish and Common languages.")
Chelaxian / Chelaxian     nothing rolled + no rank scaling
```

The culture case is structural, not an oversight: ch. 3 states outright that a culture root "always
consists of just a language." That is incompatible with the Root Rule as written in `CLAUDE.md`, so
one of the two documents needs to move. The two heritage roots used to satisfy the rule (the old
sheets had "rising to +2 at Expert rank…"); v1.8 simplified that away.

## 5. Other things worth your attention

- **IP hygiene.** `CLAUDE.md` says no Golarion proper nouns ship. v1.8 still uses Keleshite,
  Chelaxian, Kelesh, Cheliax, and Padishah Empire, so they are still in the data. Varisian is gone,
  which is progress. Say the word and I'll do a rename pass on the two surviving cultures.
- **Skills the PHB uses but never defines.** Its own rules reference Stealth (Hide, Sneak, the
  Ambusher's Training), Deception (Feint), Intimidation (Demoralize), and Diplomacy, but ch. 6 prints
  no constellation for any of them. I kept all four in `roster.json`'s `skillRoster` so they resolve
  as placeholder trees; without that, the Ambusher's granted Training has nowhere to go. Medicine,
  Nature, Society, Survival, and Thievery are referenced nowhere and were dropped.
- **No ranged combat at all.** v1.8 prints no bows, no crossbows, and no Archery or Thrown style,
  though `thrown X` survives as a weapon trait and Weapon Focus lists "bows" as a group. Archery's
  nine talents are in the backup if you want them back.
- **Great Weapons** is named in the Combat Styles intro ("a polearm sweep trips with Great Weapons")
  but has no entry in the table and no tree. Currently not a constellation.
- **Martial weapons have no proficiency path** except Weapon Focus, so a martial weapon in untrained
  hands is +0 proficiency. Consistent, but check it's intended.
- **Languages** pruned to the three the PHB names: Common, Kelish, Chelaxian. The old "Diabolic" is
  now "Chelaxian", matching ch. 3. I wrote one-line descriptions for all three (they were "TBD").
- **Mira was rebuilt.** Varisian no longer exists, so she is now Human / Versatile Human /
  Keleshite / Acrobat / Ambusher, 17 talents, all four attributes lit, HP 20. Her old Light Armor and
  Arcane Tattoos are gone with their constellations.

## 6. Follow-ups only you can do

These sit in files `CLAUDE.md` puts off-limits to me. Each is small; I've written the exact change.

1. **`assets/constellation_template.html` line 448** hides five of twenty constellations. Its nav
   list omits the `Save` and `Weapon` categories, so Awareness, Evade, Guard, Endure, and Weapons
   never appear in the Observatory viewer. This bug predates v1.8 (it hid Fortitude/Reflex/Will/
   Weapons before), but it matters far more now that the Defenses are core.
   ```js
   const order=["Skill","Save","Weapon","Combat Style","Calling","Ancestry","Culture","Heritage","Background"];
   ```
   Its `catHue` map and glyph table also still hold Archery / Light Armor / Unarmored entries and
   have nothing for Evade / Guard / Endure, which will fall back to the default colour.

2. **`assets/xlsx_to_trees.py` line 69.** `CATEGORIES` has no `Defense`, so `defenses.xlsx` has to
   declare its four trees as category `Save`. The app relabels them to "Defense" on screen, but the
   authoring sheet reads wrong. Adding `"Defense"` to the tuple (and to `FALLBACK_NEEDED`) would make
   the data honest end to end; I'd then flip the four Category cells.

3. **`assets/xlsx_to_trees.py` writes JSON without an encoding.** On Windows (Python 3.13, cp1252)
   `json.dump(out, open(OUT, "w"), …)` crashes on the `↺` in talent names. `PYTHONUTF8=1` works
   around it; `open(OUT, "w", encoding="utf-8")` on lines 288, 291, and 294 would fix it properly.

4. **`assets/sheet_gen.py`** reads `SPEC["defense"]["saves"][i]` for `i in range(3)` and maps them to
   PDF fields `fort` / `ref` / `will`. I left a four-entry `saves` key in `sheet_spec.json` so it
   still runs, but the generated PDF will show only three Defenses and drop Endure. It also needs the
   zone-armor rows, and its hardcoded Mira block (line ~271) still names Varisian, Light Armor, and
   "saves x3".

5. **`assets/build_phb.js`** still hardcodes the old ancestry list and names retired talents in
   Mira's sample build. Ask me for replacement copy and I'll write it for you to merge.

6. **Print plates.** `render_constellations.py` needs a run: 3 plates are missing (`evade`, `guard`,
   `endure`) and 16 are orphaned and safe to delete: archery, elvenkind, fortitude, heavy-armor,
   humankind, light-armor, medium-armor, mountainblood, orcblood, reflex, stealth, survival,
   thievery, unarmored, varisian, will. The 17 surviving plates all show stale talent lists and
   should be re-rendered too.
