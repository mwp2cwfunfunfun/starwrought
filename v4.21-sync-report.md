# v4.21 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.15 to v4.21, synced 2026-11-04. Mike's `StarWrought_v4.21_Reorganized_Redline.docx`
(4 November; cover and footers already v4.21; five reviewer comments in the margin and, despite the
name, no tracked changes) is the edition, run through `assets/phb_format.py` to
`Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.21.docx` with the visible text unchanged by a character. v4.16,
v4.17 and the 29 October v4.20 draft came and went on the shelf unsynced; against v4.15 the delta
is 143 changed blocks, 884 lines added and 574 removed, and whole new areas: five caster Callings,
five magical disciplines with ten specialties, the Cast Maneuver, Zoned and Pervasive Delivery, a
new damage order, same-type stacking, the Combat Styles demoted to groups, the Reactions moved into
Talents, a new armor table with Types and traits, weapon dice by rank, and a Comet in place of the
Deferred Talent Point. Mike's instruction for this and every sync after it: plan first, change
after approval (`eager-wobbling-possum.md`, approved before the first edit). Everything downstream
moved: `data/*.xlsx` and the new `data/magic.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`, the converter, the
builders, the web app, the Foundry system (0.11.0), its `FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`, both
READMEs and `CLAUDE.md`; `data/SYNC.json` is stamped v4.21.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook leaves something to the implementation, the
decisions Mike made during the sync, the defects in the book for him to settle, and what the
system does not yet automate. The numbering continues the v4.14 report's, which ended at 90, and
the system's own rulings since (91 to 124 in the changelog entries 0.7.0 to 0.10.0): this report
begins at 125.

## Mike's decisions (2026-11-04)

- The edition is v4.21, synced in one pass, magic included.
- The five casters' Opening Vigor is their root texts' (Spellshaper 6, Blood Mage 8, Necromancer
  6, Summoner 6, Spellblade 8), and the Chapter 3 Calling table is corrected by a redline:
  `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.21_callings-vigor_proposal.docx` (ruling 152).
- Accepted by silence on the recommendations: the Endure Bonus to Vigor is dropped, since Endure
  Training no longer grants it (ruling 151); the Deferred counter becomes a Comets counter (ruling
  145); spells are authored as structured columns on the magic sheets, the Aura column's precedent,
  not as a grammar kind.

## What the book now says

Quoted from the v4.21 edition, since every ruling below interprets one of these sentences.

- Bonuses and Penalties: "Independent bonuses and penalties stack, regardless of type. Count each
  underlying circumstance only once, even if multiple rules refer to it." and "If multiple rules
  describe the same benefit or penalty, apply only its strongest applicable version. The GM
  resolves uncertain cases."
- Key Terms, Attribute Bonus: "An Attribute Bonus equals the number of associated Attribute Points
  divided by 4, rounded down." No maximum is named anywhere in the edition.
- The Attack, the Strike Attribute: "Melee: Might, or Agility if the weapon has the Finesse trait.
  Ranged: Agility, or Might if the weapon has the Thrown trait." and "A Strike made as part of
  another Maneuver still uses the appropriate Melee or Ranged Proficiency. If that Maneuver's Key
  Attribute has a higher bonus than the Strike's normal Attribute, you may use it instead, provided
  you meet all of the Maneuver's Requirements."
- Weapon Damage Dice: one die at Trained, two at Expert, three at Master, four at Legendary for a
  Deliberate or Committed Strike; one on a Quick Strike or a Graze; "do not add them a second time
  as weapon specialization".
- Damage Resolution, the seven steps: Immunity; the total; all applicable Resistance; all
  applicable Weakness, only if damage remains; the Zone's Protection once for a Zoned Attack, never
  below 1; double on a Critical Hit; apply to Temporary Vigor then Vigor. "Pervasive Attacks and
  Persistent Damage ignore ordinary Protection."
- Support: "+1 Situation bonus to melee Attacks against that target, to a maximum Support bonus of
  +4."
- Rush ❷: "Move up to three times your Speed (less your Load Strain) in a straight line. You are
  Off-Guard until your next Opportunity." Leap ❶ to ❸: half your Speed, your Speed, one and a half
  times your Speed after a run of at least half of it, each less Load Strain. Crawl ❶: "While prone,
  Move up to half your Speed."
- Load and Load Strain: "Load Strain is the total Load of the armor you wear and any shield you
  carry, reduced by 1 for a Fitted Harness and reduced by your Endure bonus, to a minimum of 0."
  "Apply your Load Strain as a Condition penalty to Stealth, Acrobatics, Climb, and Swim checks."
  Wind: "If your Load Strain is at least 1, then at the end of every round while in an encounter,
  you must roll Endure against 10 + Load Strain": Failure, Fatigued +1; Critical Failure, Fatigued
  +2.
- Fatigued N: "−N Condition (maximum of 3) penalty to all Defense (Awareness, Endure, Evade, Guard)
  and Attack rolls ... Cannot Sustain spells. Ends after ten minutes of rest."
- Armor Traits: Visored, "Can Raise or Lower the visor as a single action. Lowered: Imposes −1 Gear
  penalty to Awareness and Initiative. Raised: Protection is reduced by 1, but no Awareness or
  Initiative penalties." Fitted, "If you wear armor with the Fitted trait in at least three Zones,
  and they have all been adjusted to you, reduce total Load Strain by 1." Muffled, "never
  contributes to Clatter".
- Vigor: "A full night's rest restores all of your Vigor." and "10 minutes spent relaxing and
  refocusing restores Vigor equal to your level * your Presence (if positive)."
- Chapter 7: "A Comet is a Talent Point that can be spent in any Constellation, whether or not it
  is Flared or already open." Milestone 4: "You gain a level ... You also gain a Comet." Milestone
  Conversion: "If no Talent you qualify for is eligible through any of your Flares, your Milestone
  Talent Point becomes a Comet."
- Spellwork, Cast ❷ to ❹: "Roll the named magical parent's proficiency with the spell's Key
  Attribute against the Defenses permitted by its Threat. The Talent determines the spell's range,
  targets, Delivery, damage type, and effects. No universal magic attack proficiency exists."
  Quick Cast: one damage die regardless of proficiency, Graze no damage unless the spell says.
  Focused Cast: Prepared, full Damage Dice by rank, Graze one die. Committed Cast: Prepared, the
  same dice plus the Talent's listed extra capability.
- Size and Defenses: Small −1 Guard, Medium nothing, Large −1 Evade (the Tiny and Huge rows of the
  v4.20 draft read −2 Guard and −2 Evade; see ruling 139).

## Rulings

125. **A Parry weapon and a raised shield both apply to Guard.** Two pieces of gear, two
     circumstances; with v4.21's stacking they add. Through 0.10.1 the better one stood.
126. **Two Scouts give +2 Initiative.** Independent allies, independent circumstances.
127. **A group's Talent counts toward the parent only once the parent's Root is owned, and cannot be
     bought before it.** The v4.10 sync's R2 kept; the creation wizard refuses it with the parent's
     Root named, and measures a group Talent's tier gate against the parent's pool.
128. **A critical with a group's Talent Flares the group; a Flared group satisfies a Milestone spend
     in it.** Flares stay per sky, and "a Flare records the particular training" is the group's
     own row lit.
129. **A Finesse weapon thrown uses Might.** The book's "Might if the weapon has the Thrown trait"
     read literally; the throw is decided at the roll, and the tooltip says so.
130. **Cover the Angle and Kneel Behind the Shield keep the book's text as their effect; the engine
     applies the bonus (Kneel's +4, its Arms Exposed) and offers Cover the Angle nowhere yet.**
     The Combat Prompt answers the defender's own Blow; an ally's redirect is the table's.
131. **Parry's "attempt to Gain Control" is the existing Control offer on a Stop**, and Riposte
     stands beside it when owned.
132. **A Reaction answers any Threat met with its Defense.** The table keys each on a Defense, not
     on a Blow; a Blast met with Evade may be Sidestepped when the Cast reaches the flow.
133. **One answer per attack: a Posture is a ⓿↺ Reaction.** A commitment is a Reaction or a
     Posture, never both (the reviewer's first comment, below).
134. **`psychic` is added beside `mental`, not renamed.** Both damage types exist; the sheets use
     the one they print.
135. **A Quick Cast may Critically Hit.** The book gives spells no Agile rule, and the Quick
     Strike's exception is the Strike's own.
136. **Spell damage adds no Might, specialization or sheet damage bonus.** The dice and the die are
     the whole of it, as the Cast rule reads.
137. **Precision stays inside the rolled total and is doubled with it**, carried in the card's
     flags as before.
138. **Crawl is half the Speed you had before the final Legs Wound.** A fighter who cannot Stand
     still has a Speed to halve.
139. **Gargantuan keeps Huge's −2 Evade, and Tiny −2 Guard.** The v4.21 table prints Small, Medium
     and Large only; the v4.20 draft's Tiny and Huge rows are kept for the two sizes it does not
     name, and Gargantuan reads as Huge.
140. **A lowered visor gives only the stated −1 Gear; raised, Protection −1 (the Full Helm's 4 reads
     3).** No other sense is read off it.
141. **Stored `scale` armor migrates to `lamellar`.**
142. **Clatter counts mail, lamellar, brigandine, splint and plate**, a Muffled piece never.
143. **Armor-Piercing stays the printed number** until the weapon tables name armor types (the
     reviewer's fourth comment, = 157).
144. **Ten minutes' rest restores level × Presence, 0 when Presence is 0 or less.** "if positive".
145. **A Comet is the renamed counter; what it buys is the book's.** Counted, not enforced, as the
     Deferred point was (ruling 94).
146. **"Prepared" remains the system's word for a ❸/❹ Maneuver's reserve.**
147. **A Background's "1 Relevant Lore" is the player's pick at creation; the sheets keep the old
     Lore as the default.**
148. **The Sneak row's trait reads Motion** (the sheet's "Move" was a typo for the trait).
149. **The material-weakness step is removed with the table.** Armor has a Type and no weakness.
150. **Wind's exemption (ruling 74) is superseded; a critical failure raises Fatigued by 2.** The
     v4.21 Wind bullet has no Endure Threshold clause.
151. **The Endure Bonus to Vigor is dropped** (Mike). Endure Training grants Load Strain relief
     alone.
152. **The new Callings' Opening Vigor is per their root texts** (Mike): 6 / 8 / 6 / 6 / 8, with
     the Chapter 3 table redlined to match.
153. **Weapon damage dice are the rank's (1 / 2 / 3 / 4), replacing the level table and the
     specialization bonus.** The rank is the one the Strike rolled, Handling's reduction included.
154. **One answer per attack, a Reaction or a Posture** (the reviewer's first comment).
155. **A player attacker rolls once and the one die is read against every defender's Threshold, a
     separate Result each** (the reviewer's second comment; the attack flow's behaviour since
     0.5.0).
156. **Two Persistent Damage expressions of one type are compared by average** (the reviewer's
     third comment); the system applies one source at a time and keeps the higher average when it
     must choose.
157. **Armor-Piercing N stands until the tables carry types** (the reviewer's fourth comment, =
     143). The fifth comment (combining divisors) touches no system arithmetic.
158. **The Attribute Bonus has no +5 cap.** v4.21's Key Terms sentence names none; the cap of
     v4.10 to v4.15 (ruling 1 of the v4.10 report) went with the old sentence.

## Defects in the handbook, for Mike

Each is the book's to settle; the system follows the reading given.

- The Chapter 3 Calling table's Vigor column disagrees with the five caster root texts (redlined,
  Mike's decision above).
- Chapter 3 counts thirteen points at creation and lists "1 Calling, 1 Combat-related
  Constellation" for the Calling, while every Calling root still says "You become Trained in
  <Skill>": the wizard and the app place fourteen, and the table leaves that point out.
- "our four Defenses", "Reashspeak", "quicky" (redlined in the consistency proposal);
  "combination or your resolve" (a run the redline tool could not split; by hand).
- "–9 below the Threshold" in the degrees text.
- The duplicate "spend immediately" paragraph in Chapter 7; the duplicated Serrovane row.
- Slip the Line's card is titled "Give Ground ❶↺"; Riposte's Requires reads "Parry ❶↺" (both
  redlined).
- Deflecting Palms' broken sentence; Spellblade's broken Special.
- Master Cut ("When you Counter") and the Example of Play still name Counter and Void, which the
  edition has removed.
- Key Terms still says Combat Styles are parents, Attribute Bonus ÷ 3 in one place, and "one die
  or one round".
- Staggered, Bleeding, Clatter (outside the Muffled row) and Sustain are used and not defined.
- Weaponmaster's Special still reads "Drilled"; Armored Fighting's Layered Guard names Scale.
- "Starwrought" casing in two places; the Cast heading splits the Attack table.
- Flowing Defense's glyph is dropped in the book's Talent name; the sheet keeps it.
- The Culture paragraph names Varisians ("an orc raised among Varisians is a Varisian"), a
  Golarion proper noun; `check_style.py` scans the handbook as advisory only and the sheets say
  Serrovane and Kestrel Reach.
- Size and Defenses: the table prints three sizes; Tiny, Huge and Gargantuan are read as ruling
  139 says.
- The Breastplate is the Plate Cuirass now; the v4.13 report's rename suggestion is answered.

## What is not automated

- **The Cast rolls outside the attack flow.** A spell is rolled at once, as a Reaction Strike is,
  against the higher of the two Defenses its Threat permits on the target; the defender does not
  commit, nothing is revealed, and an adversary's number prints as 0.4.2 printed it. The first
  non-Blow Threat in the coordinator is the split `CLAUDE.md` promised, and it is the next piece of
  work, not this one.
- **Spell effects beyond the die**: areas, pushes, conditions on a Hit, summons, Temporary Vigor
  pools, healing and a second damage type are the Talent's text on the card.
- **Cover the Angle** (an ally's Blow against your Guard), **Kneel's rooted Speed**, the **"no more
  than one size larger"** clauses of Parry and Deflecting Palms, **Deflecting Palms' damage
  reduction** and a Reaction Talent under a name the table does not know (offered only as a ⓿↺
  Posture by the general rule): the table's.
- **Sleeping in armor** (Fatigued equal to Load Strain, maximum 3, without Comfort) is still not
  applied; the party's rest dialog names who did.
- **Flares on groups** record the row lit and nothing more; "the particular training" is the row.
