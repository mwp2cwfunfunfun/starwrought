# v4.14 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.13 to v4.14, synced 2026-10-02, the same day as v4.11, v4.12 and v4.13. Mike
accepted the Attended redline from `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.13_attended_proposal.docx` and
streamlined the Roll Playing Conventions paragraph and the two Result tables in tracked changes of
his own, saved as `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.14.docx`; the integrator accepted those changes
through Word and wrote the edition under the same file name (cover `PLAYTEST EDITION v4.14`),
mending two slips on the way (ruling 90). No rule moved: the player still rolls, a Threshold is
still ten plus the modifier it stands in for, and the Attack results say in fewer words what they
said before. Alongside the edition came Mike's patch list for the system: the Flare lists, a Flare
from the sheet going unannounced, a party sheet question, a handbook formatting pass (a separate
workflow, which may land as v4.15 with no rules change), and the Strike Attribute question.
Everything downstream moved where it had to: `assets/roster.json`, the web app, the Foundry system
(0.6.3), its `FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`, both READMEs and `CLAUDE.md`. No world migration:
nothing stored changes shape.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook leaves something to the implementation, and
the questions for Mike. The numbering continues the v4.13 report's, which ended at 83.

## What the book now says

Quoted exactly from the v4.14 edition, because every ruling below interprets one of these sentences.

- Chapter 2, Game Conventions, Roll Playing Conventions (the sync brief's "Checks chapter"), now one
  paragraph: "The Player always rolls to determine Success. Instead of the GM rolling dice secretly
  behind a screen, the player always rolls the dice to determine if something succeeded or failed.
  This means that monsters typically have Thresholds for most of their stats, for example,
  Initiative Threshold, Claws Strike Threshold, Guard Threshold, Evade Threshold, etc. A Threshold
  is always ten plus the modifier of the check it stands in for: 10 + Attribute Bonus + Proficiency
  Bonus, plus any bonuses and penalties that apply. A creature (including a character) whose Guard
  is +7 therefore has a Guard Threshold of 17. If two players are opposed (e.g., one player attacks
  another player), the player initiating the maneuver rolls." The four short paragraphs that
  followed it in v4.13 ("The player rolls to determine success. Always. ...", "You act. ...",
  "Something comes at you. ...", "If two players are opposed (e.g., hiding from another player),
  the acting player rolls.") are gone.
- Checks & Thresholds, Degrees of Success: the lead-in reads "See the table below for more details
  (for Attacks, see the next section):", and the table has two columns, Result and "Meaning for
  most Checks". The "Meaning for Attacks" column of v4.13 is gone, with its cells ("Critical Hit:
  Extra damage, plus critical effects", "Hit: Normal damage", "Graze: Stopped, but it skates. One
  die, or one round", "Miss: Stopped. Nothing lands"). Its last row still reads "Natural 20 / 1".
- The Exchange, 3. The Result, the table (the attacker's roll against the Defense Threshold, the
  defender's roll against the Attack Threshold, and one Result):
  - "Critical Hit. Double damage, or the effect's critical result, plus critical effects. For a
    Blow, the attacker chooses the Zone that is struck."
  - "Hit. Full damage or effect. For a Blow, lands on the Torso, or on an Exposed Zone the attacker
    chooses."
  - "Graze. Stopped, but partial damage: one weapon die, or one round of effect. A Blow deals one
    weapon die to a Zone the defender chooses; a Blast deals one of its dice; a Blight or
    Beguilement takes hold only until the end of the round. Position changes in the defender's
    favor (see 5. Position)."
  - "Miss. Stopped, no damage. For a Blow, the attacker is Exposed in a Zone of the defender's
    choice."
  - The last row's heading is "Natural 20 / Natural 1" (capital N both); the defender's column of
    that row reads "Natural 1 / natural 20", lowercase on the second.
- The Exchange, 1. The Attack, Strikes, the paragraph that moved here: "Only a Committed Strike has
  the Weighted trait: a Graze against it Exposes you. A Miss Exposes you regardless of commitment;
  you Missed outright, and everyone saw where your weapon ended up. A Parry can take Control of any
  Deliberate or Committed blade it Stops, because a committed blade is there to be bound. A Quick
  Strike is a probe: Stopped, it forms at most a neutral Bind. Agile weapons are light enough to
  bite on the way in: a Quick Strike with one may Critically Hit." (The sync brief quoted the third
  sentence as "there to be caught" and stopped there; the edition says "bound" and carries two more
  sentences. This report quotes the edition.)
- The Exchange, 1. The Attack, unchanged since v4.10 and quoted because Mike asked whether it is
  implemented: "Your Strike Attribute is the higher of two numbers: the weapon's natural Attribute
  (Might; or Agility for a weapon with the Finesse trait, and for any ranged weapon other than a
  composite bow or a thrown weapon), or the Key Attribute of a Combat Style whose root you have and
  whose weapons you are wielding. Work it out once per weapon and write it on your sheet. Damage
  still adds Might where the Strike allows it."
- Chapter 5, the armor traits table, the Comfort row: "You can sleep in it without increasing your
  Fatigued value by 1." The Attended row above it, "It fastens behind the shoulder, beyond your own
  reach: alone, putting it on takes twice as long. Taking it off does not.", the Breastplate's
  Traits cell, "Plate, Noisy, Attended", and the donning paragraph's sentence, "An Attended piece
  takes that long only with a second pair of hands; alone, putting it on takes twice as long,
  though it comes off in the usual time.", are as the v4.13 proposal wrote them, accepted.
- Nothing else in the book changed. The Wind, Fatigued and Vigor rules are v4.13's, and so is the
  prose of Reading the Result for a Blow, which still says "Graze. The defender chooses the Zone the
  blow skated across" and "Miss. Nothing lands.", as the Key Terms entry for Graze still says "it
  skates".

## Rulings

84. **The streamlined Checks text moves the siblings, not the system.** The web app's Checks wiki
    carries the book's results wording, loses its "Meaning for Attacks" column and tells the reader
    where Attacks are read (its Combat section, since the app is not the book and has no "next
    section"); that Combat section's Attack results table and rows take the book's new sentences,
    the "Natural 20 / Natural 1" row and the Weighted paragraph as the book now phrases it; and the
    roster's hand-kept `armorTraits` Comfort row takes the book's sentence verbatim, "You can sleep
    in it without increasing your Fatigued value by 1.", so the Rules Reference journal, the
    Constellation Compendium and the web app's armor table all print it. No system rule moved: the
    player rolls, a player-controlled attacker rolls against the defender's Threshold, a Threshold
    is 10 plus the modifier, `weighted` is on the Committed Strike alone and `exposeOnMiss` on every
    Strike, all as 0.6.2 left them. The smoke test gains a v4.14 step (the Comfort row verbatim, no
    Attacks column in the Checks table, "partial damage" and "Stopped, no damage" in the Combat
    section, no "it skates", "Nothing lands" or "the die is in your hand" in any wiki section).
85. **Flare lists show Opened Constellations by default.** Mike: "Whenever a Flare Constellation
    button is clicked (e.g., in chat log as a result of a roll), the drop-down list should only show
    Opened Constellations. There should be a toggle, though, to instead show non-Opened
    Constellations to Flare. Based on the Character Sheet UI, I think we should also do the same
    thing on the Constellations tab." A Constellation is *Opened* for a character when they hold its
    Constellation Item or any Talent in it (the entries the data model's `#prepareConstellations`
    builds from Items); a Constellation drawn only because it is Flared is listed too, as today.
    **The chat card's dialog** (`flareFromCard` in `documents/chat.mjs`) lists Opened Constellations
    only, with the rolled one preselected, and a checkbox under the select, "Show Constellations you
    have not opened", that rebuilds the options with every shipping Constellation added
    (`enabledConstellations()`, ruling 61) and each unopened one marked "(not opened)"; when the
    rolled Constellation is itself unopened the checkbox starts ticked, so the preselection is in
    view; untick it and an unopened pick falls back to the rolled Constellation when that one is
    opened. The Lore template is left out of the unopened list, as the Relevant Check picker leaves
    it out: a Lore a character opens is always Lore (X). **The sheet's Constellations tab** lists
    Opened and Flared Constellations as today and gains a toolbar toggle, "Show every
    Constellation" (`toggleUnopened`; it reads "Opened only" while the rows are shown), that adds
    every shipping Constellation the character has not opened as a slim row in its own category,
    trailing the opened ones by name (name, Key Attribute glyph, Untrained, a "not opened" mark with
    a hint, the Flare control and the roll link; no Talent list, no caret), visibly dimmed
    (`sw-unopened`), so an unopened Constellation can be rolled or Flared from the sheet; a Flared
    one is always an entry of the data model's, so an unopened row is never lit. The toggle is the
    client setting `showUnopenedConstellations` (scope client, Boolean, default false, no entry in
    Configure Settings; the button reads and writes it, and its onChange redraws the Constellations
    part of every character sheet the client has open), so it holds across sheets and sessions for
    that user. Through 0.6.2 the dialog listed the character's Constellations and every shipping one
    together in one list, and the sheet had no way to Flare a Constellation it did not draw.
86. **A Flare goes to chat from every entry point.** Mike: "Flaring a Constellation by clicking on
    the Flare button on the charsheet in Foundry doesn't send that to chat, like changing other
    things does." `SwActor#toggleFlare(slug, state)` posts the Flare card itself after the update,
    with `ChatMessage.getSpeaker({actor})`, public, on the client that made the change: the existing
    card ("{name} is Flared. A Milestone Talent Point may be spent there, and it stays Flared until
    you do.") when a Constellation is lit, and a one-line card, "{name} is no longer Flared", when
    it is put out. `flareFromCard` calls `toggleFlare(slug, true)` and posts nothing of its own, so
    a Flare from a card is said once; putting out a Flare that was never lit writes nothing and says
    nothing. The audit of hand edits (`documents/audit.mjs`) is left alone: Flares are an object,
    not a watched field, and the Flare card is the announcement.
87. **The composite bow.** The book excepts "a composite bow or a thrown weapon" from the ranged
    weapons that read Agility; through 0.6.2 `attackAttribute` in `data/item.mjs` gave every ranged
    weapon Agility. `SwWeaponData`'s trait parser gains `composite` (a Trait "Composite", read
    case-insensitively like the others, `composite: false` in the defaults), and the natural
    Attribute becomes: Agility for a Finesse weapon and for a ranged weapon that is not Composite;
    Might otherwise. A Thrown weapon already reads as Might, because a weapon thrown from the hand is
    not `isRanged`. The comment and the item sheet's hint for the Style field say the rule;
    `FEATURES.md` says it under the Strike Attribute. The data has no composite bow, so
    `data/equipment.xlsx` is untouched: Mike authors one when he wants it, with "Composite" in its
    Traits, and the converter needs no change.
88. **The Strike Attribute, verified.** Mike: "Do we have this implemented in Foundry? If not,
    let's plan on how to implement it." It is. `strikeAttributeFor` in `data/actor.mjs` takes the
    higher of the weapon's natural Attribute (`attackAttribute`) and the Key Attribute of a Combat
    Style whose Root the character owns and whose slug the weapon's `system.style` field names; that
    Combat Style field is where "write it on your sheet" lives; the sheet's weapon row shows the
    glyph of the Attribute that won with a tooltip naming the Style; and damage still adds Might
    where the Strike allows it, since the comparison is the attack roll's alone. The one gap was the
    composite bow (ruling 87). No code moves beyond that; `FEATURES.md` quotes the sentence and says
    where each clause lives.
89. **A party sheet is planned, not built.** Mike: "Is there a built-in 'party sheet' in Foundry to
    do things like award Milestones, see various party-wide things like everyone's Skills, add Loot
    for party members to grab, allow players to choose and roll Exploration Mode activities, etc.?"
    Foundry core (v13 and v14) has no party sheet. The "Party" is a core concept only in the sense
    of the players' assigned characters (the Players list, each `game.users` entry's `character`);
    the features Mike names come from systems that build their own (pf2e's Party actor, dnd5e's
    Group actor). The plan, recorded in `CLAUDE.md` under Known outstanding work: a `party` Actor
    type in this system with (a) a members list of linked character Actors; (b) a Milestone award
    button that writes one Milestone to every member and posts a chat card; (c) a Skills grid, every
    member's rank and bonus for each Skill Constellation and Defense, read live; (d) a Loot panel,
    Items on the party Actor, where a member's owner clicks Take and the Item moves to their
    character with a card announcing it; (e) an Exploration Mode panel where each member picks an
    Activity from the actions compendium's Exploration group and rolls its check from the party
    sheet. Not built in 0.6.3; go or not is asked below.
90. **Two slips mended in the edition.** Mike's tracked changes carried "characyer" (now
    "character") and a Comfort row reading "You can sleep in it without waking increasing your
    Fatigued value by 1." (now "without increasing your Fatigued value by 1."). Both are mended in
    the edition as written and flagged here so the correction is his to confirm; the roster's
    Comfort row carries the mended sentence (ruling 84).

## Questions for the author

- **"For a Blow, lands on the Torso" has no subject.** The Result table's Hit row reads "Hit. Full
  damage or effect. For a Blow, lands on the Torso, or on an Exposed Zone the attacker chooses."
  Read with "it", the sentence also drops the qualifier the prose keeps: Reading the Result for a
  Blow says the Exposed Zone is the attacker's "if the Strike was Deliberate or Committed", and
  Table 9 holds a Quick Strike to the Torso. The system holds it to the Torso. Two words in one
  cell.
- **"The player initiating the maneuver rolls": confirm the reading.** The system's attack flow
  (ruling 65) has a player-controlled attacker roll Attack once against every defender's
  Threshold, a player-controlled defender of an adversary's Blow roll Defense against the Attack
  Threshold, and player against player never opposed: the attacking player rolls, the defending
  player's committed Threshold answers. v4.13's "the acting player rolls" and v4.14's "the player
  initiating the maneuver rolls" both read as that rule; a word confirms it, since the sentence is
  the one the flow stands on.
- **The party sheet: go or not?** Ruling 89 records the plan (members, Milestone award, Skills
  grid, Loot, Exploration Mode) and nothing is built. It is a release of its own if it goes.
- **"Natural 1 / natural 20"**, lowercase on the second, in the defender's column of the Result
  table's last row, against "Natural 20 / Natural 1" in the attacker's. One cell; the formatting
  pass may catch it.
- **The two voices of the Result.** The table now says "Graze. Stopped, but partial damage" and
  "Miss. Stopped, no damage", while Reading the Result for a Blow still says "the Zone the blow
  skated across" and "Miss. Nothing lands.", and Key Terms says a Graze "skates". They agree on the
  rule; whether the detail prose should take the table's words is yours. The web app's wiki takes
  the table's (ruling 84).
- **Rename the Breastplate to Cuirass?** Floated in the v4.13 report and still open: Attended
  describes a back-and-breast harness fastened at the shoulder, which is a cuirass by name, and the
  opening fiction already puts Hrolda in one. One cell in the Armor table; not ours to make.
- **The donning paragraph's lowercase "comfort"** ("unless your torso piece has comfort") beside
  the Comfort row and the Fatigued N condition; the formatting pass's to settle.
- The v4.10 report's questions stand in v4.14, each checked against the edition: Key Terms says
  Attribute Bonus is points "divided by 3", that a Defense is one of four Constellations "every
  character begins play Trained in", and names Varisians; Drilled says "Weapons Proficiency Bonus";
  Table 9's Quick row says a Miss costs "Nothing" (and Key Terms' Weighted entry says "a Quick
  Strike never", against the Strikes paragraph's "A Miss Exposes you regardless of commitment");
  Melee Training grants Counter beside Intercept; Aid is "Aid ❶ (⓿↺)" in the book and a single
  action in the sheet; the Example of Play still sums at d20+6; Armored Fighting is "Combat Style
  • Presence" in its heading and Might in the Combat Styles table.
