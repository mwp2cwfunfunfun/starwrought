# v4.13 sync report

Player's Handbook v4.12 to v4.13, synced 2026-10-02, the same day as v4.11 and v4.12. A point
release of the rules. Mike rewrote the Wind bullet of Load and Load Strain in the v4.12 file on the
shelf and dictated the clause; the numbered edition `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.13.docx` was
written with `assets/phb_edit.py` (EDITIONS "4.13"). He also answered the v4.12 report's questions:
the two trimmed sentences were deliberate; Fatigued should end only with ten minutes' rest; and
"Yes to all" on the armor-help design, which lands as the Attended trait. He enabled Awareness
Training in `data/defenses.xlsx`, so the Awareness Constellation ships with its Root alone and every
Defense now ships. Everything downstream moved: `data/equipment.xlsx`, `assets/roster.json`,
`assets/sheet_spec.json`, the web app, the Constellation Compendium, the Foundry system (0.6.2), its
`FEATURES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`, both READMEs and `CLAUDE.md`. No world migration: nothing stored
changes shape.

This report lists the rulings made where the handbook leaves something to the implementation, and
the questions for Mike. The numbering continues the v4.12 report's, which ended at 78.

## What the book now says

Quoted exactly from the v4.13 edition, because every ruling below interprets one of these sentences.

- Chapter 5, Load and Load Strain, the Wind bullet: "Wind. If your Load Strain is at least 1 and
  your Endure Threshold is less than 10 + your Load Strain, then at the end of every round while in
  an encounter, you must roll Endure against 10 + Load Strain. On a failure your Fatigued rises by
  1." Two changes against v4.12: the "Load Strain is at least 1" gate is back in the sentence, and
  the third-round delay ("at the end of the third round of an encounter and every round after") is
  gone; the check comes at the end of every round, from the first.
- Everything else the v4.12 report quoted stands unchanged: the Fatigued row of the Conditions
  table ("−N Condition (maximum of 3) penalty to Evade, Guard, and Attack rolls; can't use
  Exploration Mode Activities. Ends after ten minutes of rest."), Rage's aftermath, Deaf to Pain,
  Sight and hearing, Speed at the margin, and the donning paragraph ("Putting on or taking off a
  single piece takes 1 minute for each point of Protection it has. A full kit takes as long as its
  pieces. You cannot sleep in armor without waking Fatigued 1 unless your torso piece has
  comfort."). The armor traits table still reads Comfort, Noisy, Quiet, and the Breastplate's
  Traits cell still reads "Plate, Noisy".
- **Pending, not in the edition.** The Attended wording goes to Mike as tracked changes in
  `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.13_attended_proposal.docx` (the `attended` proposal of
  `assets/phb_propose.py`). In the armor traits table, a row before Comfort: "Attended. It fastens
  behind the shoulder, beyond your own reach: alone, putting it on takes twice as long. Taking it
  off does not." In the donning paragraph, after "A full kit takes as long as its pieces.": "An
  Attended piece takes that long only with a second pair of hands; alone, putting it on takes twice
  as long, though it comes off in the usual time." And the Breastplate's Traits cell: "Plate,
  Noisy, Attended". The data and the system carry all three today, at Mike's word; the book carries
  them once he accepts.

## Rulings

79. **The Wind check comes at the end of every round.** The v4.13 sentence says "at the end of
    every round while in an encounter", where v4.11 and v4.12 said "at the end of the third round
    of an encounter and every round after". The system follows the sentence: `WIND_ROUND` in
    `config.mjs` is 1, so the first Wind card posts at the end of round 1, for a fighter with Load
    Strain 1 or more whose Endure Threshold is below 10 + Load Strain and who is not yet Fatigued
    3, unconscious or Dying, and every round after. Nothing else about the check moves: the
    exemption (ruling 74) and its live reading, the ceiling of 3, the card's Endure button and the
    Load Strain tooltip are as 0.6.1 left them. Every string, comment and document that said "the
    third round" now says the end of every round: `lang/en.json` (the Wind hint, the sheet's
    tooltip, the Wind card), the Combat's docblocks, the web app's sheet line ("Wind each round:
    Endure vs M") and wiki, the printed sheet's Defense ladder line ("each round"), the
    Constellation Compendium's armor paragraph, `FEATURES.md`, the READMEs and `CLAUDE.md`. The
    delay was the one thing that made the first two rounds of a fight free for an armored fighter;
    its loss is flagged to Mike below, in case the rewrite dropped it by accident.
80. **The Strain clause is the book's.** "If your Load Strain is at least 1" is back in the
    sentence, which is the clause the v4.12 report asked for: without it, read literally, an
    unarmored fighter whom Frightened pulled below Endure Threshold 10 would roll for Wind. The
    system had kept the gate as its own guess since v4.12 dropped the clause (ruling 74), so
    nothing moves in code; the question is closed, and the documents now cite the sentence rather
    than defending the gate.
81. **Fatigued ends with ten minutes' rest, and the table says when.** Mike: "it should only go
    away with a 10 minutes' rest." Ruling 70 (v4.11) cleared Fatigued when the Combat document was
    deleted, as the nearest thing to "ten minutes of rest once the fight is over"; v4.12 cut the
    fight from the sentence and the v4.12 report asked whether the clear should go. It goes.
    `onCombatEnds` in `documents/combat.mjs` keeps only its own housekeeping (the local pass
    streaks), and a fighter who leaves one fight Fatigued carries it into the next scene until
    someone rests. Two things clear it. **A night's rest** (`restForTheNight` in
    `documents/actor.mjs`) clears Fatigued through `setCondition("fatigued", false)`, since ten
    minutes of rest is less than a night, and the Rest card says so in a second sentence when it
    did. And **the Fatigued card** a failed Wind check posts carries a "Ten minutes' rest" button
    (`windRest`), for the actor's owner or the GM, which sets Fatigued off and posts a one-line card
    to the table ("{name} has caught their breath: Fatigued ends."); clicked on a fighter who is no
    longer Fatigued, it gives a quiet notification and does nothing else. The judgement (did ten
    minutes pass between this scene and the next?) sits with the table and not in a formula, which
    is where the working agreement puts GM judgement. The token palette toggle still works, and the
    Fatigued hint says the condition ends after ten minutes of rest, through the rest card or the
    card's button. Ruling 70 is superseded.
82. **Attended, on the Breastplate, display only.** Mike's "Yes to all" on the armor-help design.
    The trait: "It fastens behind the shoulder, beyond your own reach: alone, putting it on takes
    twice as long. Taking it off does not." Data: the Breastplate's Traits cell on the Armor sheet
    of `data/equipment.xlsx` reads "Plate, Noisy, Attended" (Material first, so the converter's
    material-from-first-trait rule stays quiet), and the roster's hand-kept `armorTraits` block
    gains the row first, alphabetically (Attended, Comfort, Noisy, Quiet); `equipment.json` and the
    roster's `armorPieces` block follow from the converter, and the Constellation Compendium, the
    Rules Reference journal and the web app's trait table all print that block. System: the armor
    Item (`SwArmorData` in `data/item.mjs`) derives `attended` (its traits include "Attended") and
    `donTimeAlone` (twice `donTime` when attended, else equal to it) beside `donTime`; the Equipment
    tab's time tag reads "4 min, 8 alone" for an Attended piece and its hint says why. Nothing else
    acts on it: no Load, Protection, Strain or check reads the trait, and the system still only
    says how long donning would take mid-fight rather than preventing it. The book does not carry
    the trait yet: the wording stands as tracked changes for Mike to accept (above), so on this one
    item the data runs a step ahead of the book, at his word, and `CLAUDE.md` lists it under Known
    outstanding work until the redline is accepted or amended.
83. **Awareness ships, with its Root alone.** Mike set Awareness Training's `Enabled?` to Yes on the
    Awareness sheet of `data/defenses.xlsx`; the other four rows stay blank. A Constellation ships
    when its Root does (ruling 60), so the Awareness Constellation and its one Talent go into the
    compendia, every one of the four Defenses is now one the wizard's Defenses step can Train, and
    the enabled counts become 13 of 31 Constellations and 39 of 177 Talents; `content/sync.json`
    carries them once the integrator's build runs, and the Foundry README and `FEATURES.md` say so.
    No code moved: the gate was already data.

## Questions for the author

- **Was dropping the third-round delay intended?** v4.11 and v4.12 made the Wind check start "at
  the end of the third round of an encounter"; v4.13 says "at the end of every round while in an
  encounter". The system follows v4.13 (ruling 79), so an armored fighter who is not exempt rolls
  Endure from the end of round 1. If the two free rounds were meant to stay, `WIND_ROUND` is one
  number in `config.mjs` and the strings say "every round" in a handful of places; a word settles
  it either way.
- **Accept or amend the Attended wording.** The trait row, the donning sentence and the
  Breastplate's cell are tracked changes in `Starwrought_Players_Handbook_v4.13_attended_proposal.docx`
  (author "Claude"). The data and the system already carry them (ruling 82). If the wording changes
  on acceptance, the roster's `armorTraits` row and the Breastplate's Traits cell follow in the next
  sync.
- **Rename the Breastplate to Cuirass?** A cuirass is, by name, a back-and-breast harness fastened
  at the shoulder and the side, which is exactly what Attended describes; the book's own opening
  fiction already puts Hrolda in a cuirass. "Breastplate" reads as the front plate alone, which a
  fighter could strap on unaided. The rename is one cell in the Armor table (and the Leather
  cuirass would want a distinguishing word) and would make the trait self-explaining; not ours to
  make.
- **The Comfort row's lowercase "fatigued".** The armor traits table still says "You can sleep in it
  without waking fatigued", the old boolean's word, while the donning paragraph says "waking
  Fatigued 1" and the Conditions table defines Fatigued N. One cell; nothing downstream acts on it
  (sleeping in armor without Comfort is still the table's rule to apply).
- The v4.10 report's questions stand in v4.13, each checked against the edition: Key Terms says
  Attribute Bonus is points "divided by 3", that a Defense is one of four Constellations "every
  character begins play Trained in", and names Varisians; Drilled says "Weapons Proficiency
  Bonus"; Table 9's Quick row says a Miss costs "Nothing"; Melee Training grants Counter beside
  Intercept; Aid is "Aid ❶ (⓿↺)" in the book and a single action in the sheet; the Example of Play
  still sums at d20+6; Armored Fighting is "Combat Style • Presence" in its heading and Might in
  the Combat Styles table.
