> longer refers to Courteous Comeback, Distracting Feint says Awareness instead of Perception, Close

## 2. Rules the app now implements

Terminology: **Heritage → Bloodline** everywhere. **Off-Guard** now means "against all foes", and the
new **Wrong-Footed [X]** means "Off-Guard against that creature only". **Pressed is gone.**

- **A Graze always Exposes a Zone**, chosen by the defender. That replaces the v1.8 Evade-yields /
  Guard-is-Pressed split entirely.
- **Exposed means that Zone's Protection counts as 0**, which makes Exposure far more dangerous than
  it was, and makes Recenter ◆ a real tempo decision.
- **Postures**: a reaction taken before you know the outcome, worth +2, and it Exposes a Zone win or
  lose. Everyone knows Give Ground (Evade) and Set Your Feet (Guard) from character creation.
- **Might is back in damage**: `[weapon dice + specialization + Might + bonuses] − Protection`.
  v1.8 had removed it. A Graze is still one weapon die and nothing else.
- **Protection can never reduce damage below 1.** Resistance and Immunity can reach 0; armor cannot.
- **Load Strain = total armor Load − your Endure reduction.** Might no longer offsets it, so heavy
  armor is much heavier: a full plate harness is Load 8 and Strain 6 at Trained Endure.
- **Matched harness** now requires the same *material* as well as the same Protection, and cuts Load
  Strain by 1 rather than Load.
- **Material weakness is −1**, not −2, and **Scale** is new: it turns nothing poorly.
- **Size** affects Evade and Guard on a five-step table (Tiny ±2 through Huge ∓2), and sets natural
  Reach. **Total Reach = natural + weapon.** The grid is 1 foot and a Step is 3 feet.
- **Dying, Wounded, and Recovery checks** are implemented and tracked on the sheet, with the Recovery
  Threshold (10 + level + Dying) computed live.
- **A night's rest restores level × Presence HP** (minimum your level).
- **Hero Points reroll any check.** v1.8 restricted them to checks you were Trained in.
- **Chargen is 12 points, not 8.** Three Origin roots granted by the choices, 2 Skill + 1 Lore from
  Background, 1 Calling plus the Training it grants, 1 Defense, 3 Comets, on top of 5 free roots.
  A finished 1st-level character has **17 Talent Points minimum**, and the wizard now enforces that.
- Nine **Talent Point types** are documented in the wiki, including Deferred.

Three new wiki sections cover the chapters v3.0 added: **Movement** (grid, size, reach, terrain),
**Awareness & Cover** (detection states, light, the four-grade cover table), and **Going Down**
(hit points, temporary hit points, dying, wounded, refusing death).

## 3. Places v3.0 contradicts itself, and how I ruled

1. **The Human root is named "Humanity", but every Human talent's Requires cell says "Human".**
   "Human" is the Constellation, not a talent, so the converter would reject it. I set the root to
   **Humanity** and pointed the dependents at it. This also means a v1.8 save's "Human" talent is
   migrated to "Humanity".

2. **Culture names.** The section headings are the places (Serrovane, Kestrel Reach); the
   Cultures-at-a-glance table's "Culture" column holds the demonyms (Serrovanian, Reacher), which are
   also the root talent names. I used **places for the Constellations, demonyms for the roots**.

3. **The Combat Styles table lists 8 styles; the chapter prints 10.** Great Weapon Fighting and
   Armored Fighting have full talent tables but are missing from the summary table. I included all
   ten. Either the table needs two rows added or those two sections need cutting.

4. **`Light on Your Feet ◆`** (Armored Fighting) has an empty Tier and an empty Requires cell.
   Defaulted to Trained, requiring Armored Fighting Training like its siblings.

5. **Archery refers to a "Volley penalty"** in Draw a Bead and Close Shot, but v3.0's trait table has
   no Volley trait: bows carry **Unwieldy [N]** instead. I rewrote both to say Unwieldy.

6. **`Reload [N]` has an empty Effects cell** in the weapon traits table. I wrote the obvious
   definition ("it takes N actions to reload before it can be fired again").

7. **Distracting Feint** penalises "Perception and Agility checks". Perception is not a term in v3.0;
   **Awareness** is. Changed.

8. **Quick Tongue**'s requirements still say "you haven't used *Courteous Comeback*", which was its
   v1.8 name. Changed to Quick Tongue.

9. **Background descriptions are literally `TBD$$$$$`** for all three. I wrote original copy for
   Soldier and Criminal and kept the v1.8 Acrobat text. **This is authored, not from the book**, and
   is the one place the app says something the PHB does not.

10. **Weaponmaster's Drilled** says you may take Reactive Strike "ignoring its requirements", but
    Reactive Strike is tier **E**, so a 1st-level Weaponmaster still cannot buy it. I implemented the
    tier gate as printed. If "requirements" was meant to include the tier gate, that needs saying.
    *Unchanged in v3.1.*

11. **Armored Fighting's Key Attribute, and this one is still open in v3.1.** The section subtitle
    says *Combat Style • Presence*; the Combat Styles summary table, which v3.1 newly added a row to,
    says **Might**. I used **Presence**, following the section heading, the same way Shield Fighting
    was resolved. Six of its thirteen talents carry a Might override, so Might is defensible: this is
    a one-cell change in `combat_styles.xlsx` if you want it the other way.

## 4. Root Rule status

Down from four violations to two, and both are Human bloodlines:

```
Human / Versatile Human   nothing rolled + no rank scaling  ("You gain 1 Opening Talent Point.")
Human / Torchbearer Human nothing rolled + no rank scaling  (allies' first Defense roll each round)
```

**The culture roots now pass.** Giving them a Diplomacy bonus that scales with the Origin
Constellation fixed the structural conflict I flagged in the v1.8 report: a culture root is no longer
"just a language". That was the one place `CLAUDE.md` and the PHB genuinely disagreed, and the PHB
moved.

## 5. Other things worth your attention

- **The Combat Styles are now the bulk of the game.** Ten styles, 61 talents, more than a third of
  everything authored. Armored Fighting alone has 13.
- **Skills lost Crafting, Occultism, and Religion.** Nothing in v3.0 references them, so they are
  gone from `skillRoster` too, and any saved character who had them loses those talents on load.
  They are in `backup/pre-v3.0/` if they were meant to survive.
- **Ranged combat exists again**: 8 ranged weapons, three ranged styles, Reload, Capacity,
  Armor-Piercing, and Mechanical (which suppresses Might on damage).
- **Mira was rebuilt** as Human / Versatile Human / Kestrel Reach / Acrobat / Ambusher, 18 talents,
  HP 20. Her kit is now four matching leather pieces, deliberately, so the sheet demonstrates a
  matched harness: +1 torso Protection and Load Strain 0.
- **`Reactive Strike` is the only non-Trained talent in the whole book.** Everything else is tier T,
  so the Expert/Master/Legendary tiers are still almost entirely unwritten.

## 6. Follow-ups — all seven now done

These were listed as "only you can do" because `CLAUDE.md` fenced off the pipeline scripts, not
because they were hard. You lifted the rule on 2026-08-27 and asked for them; all seven are fixed.

1. **`assets/constellation_template.html`** — the sidebar hid the `Save` and `Weapon` categories, so
   all four Defenses and Weapons were unreachable in the Observatory viewer. Rather than adding two
   strings to the whitelist, the ordering now lists preferred categories first and then appends
   **any category the data has that the list forgot**, so a new category can never silently vanish
   again. Retired spellings (`Save`, `Heritage`) fold into the current word instead of forming a
   second bucket. Its colour table and all 30 constellation glyphs were rebuilt; sixteen were missing.

2. **`assets/xlsx_to_trees.py`** — `Defense` and `Bloodline` are now real categories, with `Save` and
   `Heritage` kept as aliases so older sheets still convert. `defenses.xlsx` now says `Defense`, and
   the generated ancestry chassis says `bloodlines`. The app keeps its alias map only to rescue a
   stale `trees.json`.

3. **The converter's JSON encoding** — `json.dump(..., open(path, "w"))` crashed on the `↺` in talent
   names under Windows cp1252, which is why every run needed `PYTHONUTF8=1`. All three writes now
   pass `encoding="utf-8"`. The same bug on the *read* side in `render_constellations.py` was
   turning `◆` into `â—†` on every plate; fixed there too.

4. **`assets/sheet_gen.py`** — the sheet now carries four Defenses (check *and* Threshold each),
   armor by Zone with Protection and an Exposed checkbox per Zone, Load / Load Strain, and
   Dying / Wounded with the Recovery Threshold spelled out. The fillable PDF gained 24 new form
   fields and dropped `ac`, `fort`, `ref`, `will`, and `armor`. Long footnotes now wrap instead of
   running off the page, and Mira's example sheet is rebuilt to v3.0.

5. **`assets/build_phb.js`** — rebuilt, and repointed. It was regenerating a full rules handbook,
   which is now a liability: the PHB is hand-authored in Word and authoritative, so a script that
   also writes rules prose creates a second source of truth that will drift. It now builds
   **`Starwrought_Constellation_Compendium.docx`** instead: the creation menus, all 30 Constellations
   with every Talent and star map, and the reference appendices (conditions, traits, actions by mode,
   equipment, thresholds), every table read straight from `trees.json` and `roster.json`. That is the
   content that genuinely must track the data, and none of it duplicates your handbook prose.

6. **Print plates** — all 30 rendered with a v3.0 colour palette, and the 20 orphans deleted
   (backed up under `backup/pre-v3.0/constellations/`). 30 trees, 30 plates, none missing.

7. **`CLAUDE.md`** — the "min +1" attribute floor is gone (the PHB's table has said 0–2 = +0 since
   v1.8). "Known outstanding work" now lists what is actually outstanding: the two Root Rule
   violations, the unwritten Expert/Master/Legendary tiers, the 8-vs-10 Combat Styles table, and the
   `TBD$$$$$` background copy. The source-of-truth section now says plainly that the handbook is
   authoritative and the spreadsheets derive from it.

**The scope rule is retired** (Mike, 2026-08-27, "I want the fence to come down for good").
`CLAUDE.md`'s Scope section no longer lists files Claude may not touch. What replaced it is a
sources-vs-generated table (hand-editing `trees.json` is pointless, not forbidden: the next run
overwrites it), the five-stage pipeline in order, and what is expected of a change rather than asked
permission for: back up before a destructive pass, leave the converter at zero errors and the smoke
test clean, actually open the app in a browser, and write the sync report. Two things still warrant a
question instead of a guess: a rules ambiguity with two defensible readings, and deleting authored
game content.
